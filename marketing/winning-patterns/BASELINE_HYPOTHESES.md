# ProofBerry Creative Pattern Library

No pattern is labeled a **winner** until measured distribution data supports it.

## Baseline hypotheses to test

### H1 — Identity reframe
Pattern: `You're already in marketing. The product is you.`
Audience: candidates
Why test: reframes job search from passive application behavior into active capability marketing; immediately differentiates from generic resume advice.
Primary metric: 3-second hold, completion rate, waitlist CTR.

### H2 — Gatekeeper / visibility
Pattern: `Your first job isn't getting hired. It's getting surfaced.`
Audience: candidates
Why test: connects the pain of ATS/AI screening directly to the need for structured signal.
Primary metric: hook retention + landing conversion.

### H3 — Resume vs human dimensionality
Pattern: `A resume is 2D. You aren't.`
Audience: candidates
Why test: simple visual metaphor with strong demonstration potential.
Primary metric: share/save rate + waitlist conversion.

### H4 — Evidence, not claims
Pattern: `A hundred resumes can say experienced. Which one can show the evidence?`
Audience: recruiters / staffing
Why test: speaks directly to triage fatigue without promising automated hiring decisions.
Primary metric: LinkedIn CTR + recruiter waitlist submissions.

### H5 — Human authority
Pattern: `AI can help surface. Humans should still decide.`
Audience: HR / talent leaders
Why test: reduces resistance to AI by framing ProofBerry as explainable decision support rather than black-box replacement.
Primary metric: qualified B2B signups + comments from HR/recruiting professionals.

### H6 — Role requirement mapping
Pattern: `Can they do the work?`
Audience: employers / workforce organizations
Why test: moves discussion away from resume aesthetics toward role-relevant evidence.
Primary metric: employer/workforce waitlist conversion.

## Promotion rule
A hypothesis is promoted to `WINNER` only after it beats the current audience/channel baseline on the primary conversion metric with enough traffic to avoid reacting to tiny samples. Store the exact hook, visual recipe, CTA, audience, channel, run dates, and measured results.
