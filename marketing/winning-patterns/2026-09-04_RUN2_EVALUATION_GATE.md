## Minimum evidence before promotion

Promote a creative recipe to `winner` only when:
1. The asset has verified publication evidence.
2. The comparison has the same audience, CTA, landing destination, and materially similar distribution window.
3. There are enough impressions/clicks to avoid declaring a winner from a tiny sample.
4. The challenger improves at least one downstream metric without materially degrading qualified conversion.
5. The result is repeatable in a second variant or second distribution window.

## Metric hierarchy

1. Qualified waitlist signup rate
2. Landing-page conversion rate
3. Qualified click-through rate
4. Video hold/retention/completion
5. Saves, shares and comments from target buyers/candidates
6. Raw views/impressions

Views alone never define a winner.
