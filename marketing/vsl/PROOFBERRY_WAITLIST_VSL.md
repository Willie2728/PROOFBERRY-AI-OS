# PROOFBERRY AI — Waitlist VSL

## Master VSL — Candidate / 75–90 seconds

**HOOK**
If you're looking for a job, you're already in marketing. The product is you — your skills, your judgment, your experience, your ability to do the work.

**PROBLEM**
But most candidates are still trying to market a three-dimensional human being with a two-dimensional document. A resume can describe what you did. It cannot fully prove what you can do. And before an employer ever speaks with you, software and automated screening may decide whether you even reach the interview.

**REFRAME**
That means your first job isn't getting hired. Your first job is getting surfaced.

**SOLUTION**
ProofBerry AI is being built for that new reality. Instead of stopping at resume words, ProofBerry is designed to help you demonstrate role-specific capability through structured evidence, skills verification, simulations, interviews, and work samples — packaged so your capability can be understood by both people and the increasingly agentic systems helping them evaluate talent.

Indeed can show you jobs. A video tool can record you talking. ProofBerry's ambition is different: become the skills-verification and capability-marketing layer between the candidate and the opportunity.

**VISION**
Your resume becomes one input — not your entire identity. Your verified capabilities become the story. Your evidence becomes the spotlight. And employers get a clearer reason to move you forward.
