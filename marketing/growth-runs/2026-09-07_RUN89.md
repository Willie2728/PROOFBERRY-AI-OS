## 1. ProofBerry AI / SYNTHSAPIENS / PAAOS

### Customer Truth
A workforce catalog is an exploration surface, not deployment evidence. A commercially stronger employer signal is a buyer who can name the organization and one bounded workflow before sharing contact information.

### Creative Strategy
**PB-TXT-099 — Workflow Before Contact**

Hook: **Don’t send your email before you can name the workflow.**

CTA: **Define one bounded workflow, then request a one-workflow scoping review.**

### Production Readiness
Implemented in Base44:
- Employer/workforce contact fields remain hidden until organization + bounded workflow are present.
- Buyer must explicitly request a one-workflow scoping review before contact is revealed.
- Changing organization/workflow resets the review request.
- Buyer-qualification metadata records scope presence and explicit review intent.
- Success copy states that the stored request is not deployment, integration, pilot acceptance, contract, response-time commitment, or execution.
- Admin commercial controls now include future `employer_workforce_early_access` requests: fit classification, follow-up state, owner, next action, due date, aging attention.
- Admin dashboard adds a separate Workforce Workflow Requests count.

A script is not an episode, and an unreleased episode concept is not audience demand. Before producing blindly, collect a low-friction preference signal about which first episode people most want.

### Creative Strategy
**TOAP-TXT-002 — Audience Signal Before Audio**

Hook: **Before we render Episode 1, tell us what deserves to be heard first.**

CTA: **Choose one topic. No email required.**

### Production Readiness

Topic selection stores only topic key, UTM attribution, and timestamp. The UI explicitly states that preference does not mean an episode is produced, scheduled, or released.

## 3. MarketFlow AI

### Customer Truth
More content is not a growth strategy. A content loop should not activate until the operator has defined the qualified action, destination, measurement plan, and decision threshold the campaign is meant to serve.

### Creative Strategy
**MF-TXT-001 — Define the Conversion Before the Content Loop**

Hook: **More content is not a growth strategy.**

CTA: **Define the conversion before you activate the content loop.**

### Production Readiness
Extended Campaign with:
- `qualified_conversion_goal`
- `destination_url`
- `measurement_plan`
- `success_threshold`
- `activation_basis=qualified_conversion`

Campaign Builder now requires all four planning fields before campaign activation. The interface explicitly states that activation starts internal content generation only and does not publish externally or prove conversion.
