### Customer Truth

### Creative Strategy
- **PB-TXT-148:** “You do not need all four proofs. You do need to know what is missing.”
- **PB-DOC-054:** Candidate Proof State Card — “Zero evidence selected can be a valid proof state.”

The candidate must deliberately review all four evidence types, but is not required to select any evidence that is not genuinely ready. A reviewed 0/4 state is valid planning information.

## 3) VERLORAY Business Generator OS

### Customer Truth
The connected pricing flow already separated a one-mission readiness brief from checkout intent, but the plan cards did not carry the mission-readiness state directly into the buying decision. A plan click without that context is weaker qualification than a plan click tied to one bounded mission.

### Creative Strategy
- **VER-TXT-013:** “The plan is not the decision. The decision is whether this one mission is ready to be evaluated.”
- **VER-DOC-001:** Mission-Ready Purchase Context.

Each pricing card now explains whether the current plan choice is tied to a mission-ready brief, a complete brief with unresolved gaps, or no brief. Checkout remains available, but payment is not framed as authorization to execute.
