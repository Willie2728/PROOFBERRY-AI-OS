## 1. Customer Truth — ProofBerry

Fresh market observation: hiring-assessment products are increasingly selling role-specific evidence, traceable scoring and human review rather than generic AI ranking alone.

Run 13 sources:
- Workera, July 28, 2026: proctored assessments built around job-mirroring tasks and evidence for high-stakes hiring review.
- FairShot: role-specific adaptive interviews with transcript-linked evidence and recruiter-owned decisions.
- The Cigna Group, March 2026: AI may assist hiring, but recruitment remains human-led and people make progression decisions.

Run 13 thesis:

**A hiring score without evidence is just another number.**

ProofBerry should sell the evidence chain, not generic scoring:

**role criterion → observed candidate evidence → structured score/context → human review.**

Durable research brief:
`marketing/customer-truth/2026-09-05_RUN13_EVIDENCE_CHAIN.md`
