## 3. CityVerse Live

### Customer Truth
**A success signal without an evidence category is not decision-ready.**

The owned first-test surface already said `One Proof Point · One Signal · One Receipt · One Dependency`, but the receipt/evidence category was optional in the UI, qualification logic, and entity schema. That was a real qualification inconsistency.

### Creative Strategy
- `CV-TXT-006` — No Receipt, No Decision-Ready Test.
- `CV-DOC-002` — First-Test Evidence Receipt Card.

### Production Readiness
RUN120 makes `required_receipt_type` part of the first-test qualification gate. Contact/private review cannot unlock until a visitor defines:
- relationship/role,
- first experience/proof point,
- second-step success signal,
- required receipt/evidence category,
- external dependency/test condition.

Required receipt categories:
- experience/viewpoint log,
- transaction/commerce receipt,
- creator/fan interaction receipt,
- rights/access confirmation,
- other evidence category.

Both `CityVerseInterest` and `CityVerseScopeSignal` now require the structured receipt category. Default content variant is `CV-TXT-006`.

Claims boundary: selecting a receipt or dependency does not mean CityVerse has live rights, feeds, inventory, payment rails, partner access, memberships, reservations, transactions, or production availability.
