## 2. VERLORAY MUSIC AI POWERED AUTONOMOUS RECORDING OS

### Customer Truth

**Do not borrow fake traction to make an AI artist concept feel real. Make the release state inspectable.**

The reviewed Base44 landing already identifies the roster as AI artist concepts, but the deep artist profile still presented concept titles as `Latest Releases` and displayed streams, fans, and `+24%` growth. RUN117 removed those cues from the reviewed surfaces.

### Creative Strategy

**VM-DOC-001 — One-Track Release Trust Card**

The reusable no-contact release-readiness asset asks for:

1. AI-use label (`AI-Generated`, `AI-Assisted`, or unresolved);
2. accountable human creative owner / approval;
3. source, sample, model/provider, performer, voice/likeness, and other rights questions as applicable;
4. recording identity / ISRC workflow when applicable;
5. actual release destination plus provider receipt before calling a track released;
6. real audience/commercial evidence only after provider or accounting receipts exist.

Claims boundary: the card is a planning aid, not legal clearance, rights certification, a distribution agreement, chart eligibility, or proof a track is live.
