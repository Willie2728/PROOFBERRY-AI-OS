## 1. ProofBerry AI / PAAOS

### Customer Truth
**A qualified request should not open a black box.** The buyer flow already helps a recruiter define a bounded one-role review and recommends one state-aware next resource. The next conversion-quality problem was what happens after the buyer presses `Request the one-role review`: a serious buyer should not have to infer whether the request is automatically accepted, scheduled, assigned an SLA, or treated as evidence of authority/budget.

### Creative Strategy
Created `PB-TXT-137 — Know What Happens After the Request`.

**Hook:** A qualified request should not open a black box.

**CTA:** Request the one-role review only when the buyer understands what the request does — and does not — trigger.

The owned CTA now states that ProofBerry records the scoped request first, a human reviews the buyer-defined role and evaluation boundaries, and follow-up can occur if there is a fit. It explicitly does not promise pilot acceptance, scheduling, response time, authority/budget verification, candidate verification, or a hiring outcome.
