## Executive decision

RUN122 optimized for **qualified traffic, qualification integrity, and interpretable buyer intent** rather than raw reach or internal activity volume.

1. **ProofBerry:** repair a real telemetry semantics defect. The UI defined “evaluation-ready” as four buyer-defined review-quality signals, but the `pilot_evaluation_ready` event and several `evaluation_ready` metadata fields could still become true after baseline selection alone. RUN122 aligned analytics with the actual 4/4 boundary.
2. **MarketFlow:** require an explicit **disqualification rule** alongside the qualified-audience definition before the internal content loop can activate. Qualified growth should define both who is in-scope and who should be excluded, suppressed, or routed elsewhere.
3. **ReelForge:** require a concrete **test-media source** before private prototype-fit review. A workflow test is easier to interpret when the buyer identifies whether it will use requester-owned footage, synthetic demo media, a licensed/cleared sample, or client-provided media with stated permission.

No video, image, or audio render was attempted or claimed. No social post, ad, email campaign, or external publication was verified live. Successful application builds were not treated as production deployments.

## 1. ProofBerry AI / PAAOS

### Customer Truth

**Do not let analytics call a pilot evaluation-ready before the buyer has defined the evaluation.**

Connected Base44 source exposed an internal mismatch. RUN120 had established four review-quality signals:

- comparison baseline
- evidence delivery boundary
- evidence retention boundary
- minimum evidence packet/rule

The visible review-quality tier correctly required all four, but `pilot_evaluation_ready` still fired when the baseline alone became ready, and four metadata payloads used `evaluation_ready: pilotBaselineReady`.

That could corrupt qualified-intent analytics by recording an evaluation-readiness state the buyer had not actually completed.

Current category context reinforces the need for more inspectable assessment design without establishing ProofBerry performance: SHRM reported on AI-enabled candidate “skillfishing” and cited analysis of nearly 20,000 interviews from July 2025 through January 2026 in which a substantial share showed signs of cheating. This is market context only; it is not evidence that ProofBerry detects, prevents, or resolves such behavior.

## 3. ReelForge

### Customer Truth

**Define the test footage before you judge the editor.**

The existing prototype-fit flow already asked for a capability category, current workflow, exact proof test, pass criterion, decision window, and creator-control requirement. It did not require the buyer to say what media would actually be used for the proof test.

That left a practical interpretation gap: a prototype might appear to pass or fail because of input-media differences rather than the editing workflow itself, and an undefined source can create unnecessary rights/privacy ambiguity.

Adobe’s June 16, 2026 Creators’ Toolkit report, based on a survey of more than 16,000 creators, found strong demand for human creative control and review/edit/undo capability in agentic AI workflows. This supports keeping creator control and inspectable test boundaries explicit; it does not establish ReelForge performance.

### Creative Strategy

**RF-TXT-007 — Define the test footage before you judge the editor**

Hook: **Define the test footage before you judge the editor.**

CTA: **Pick the test-media source before requesting a prototype-fit review.**

- Commit: `04367a38191e95ac4b2722bdce2277c02c3d0cc0`

### Production Readiness

Base44 `src/pages/ReelForge.jsx` now requires a concrete `test_asset_source` before `scopeReady` becomes true and before private review can unlock. Structured source classes:

- requester-owned footage
- synthetic/generated demo media

A `to_define` UI state is allowed for scoping but does not unlock private review and does not create an anonymous signal/request.

`PrototypeScopeSignal` and `PrototypeFitRequest` schemas now include the structured `test_asset_source` field. The anonymous scope signal stores only the source category, not the media file or free-text rights details.

The UI and copied brief state that the selected source is requester-supplied context, not independently verified ownership, license, permission, copyright clearance, render proof, export proof, or publishing proof.
