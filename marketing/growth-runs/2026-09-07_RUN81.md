# 3. VerifyTrust

## Customer Truth
Identity verification starts with an assurance target and a bounded workflow — not a green checkmark.

NIST SP 800-63A-4 describes identity proofing as a process in which an applicant provides evidence that lets a credential service provider assert identity at a useful identity assurance level, with requirements for three IALs. This supports scoping the required assurance and evidence path first; it does not establish VerifyTrust conformance or certification.

Sources:
- https://www.nist.gov/publications/nist-sp-800-63a-4digital-identity-guidelines-identity-proofing-and-enrollment
- https://pages.nist.gov/800-63-4/sp800-63a.html
