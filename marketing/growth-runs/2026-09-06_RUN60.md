## 2. CityVerse

### Customer Truth
The product already disclosed prototype status in some surfaces, but AURA and Shop The Moment still presented specific events, brands, prices, ratings, payment methods and checkout behavior like verified live inventory. That inconsistency weakens trust and could misdirect traffic toward fictional inventory instead of the real product thesis.

### Creative Strategy
Campaign: **Experience Before Inventory**

Hook: **Sell the feeling of being there — not inventory you have not verified.**

CTA: Explore the CityVerse experience model and identify which event category or POV is worth validating first.

## 3. VERLORAY

### Customer Truth
A draft governance policy can describe what is allowed without proving that the connector, credential, permission, spending limit, approval rule and runtime required to execute that action are actually configured. Pricing copy also used unsupported popularity and live billing-state language.

### Creative Strategy
Campaign: **Policy Is Not Capability**

Hook: **A policy can authorize an action. Only a verified runtime can prove the action exists.**

CTA: Inspect the control plane first. Then connect only the capabilities you are prepared to authorize.

- Added explicit note: draft policy surface is not a capability receipt.
