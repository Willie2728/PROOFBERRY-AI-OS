# 2. PRINT MY STORY AI

## Customer Truth
For a personalized gift, the meaningful pre-purchase signal is not just a CTA click. The stronger signal is whether the buyer reads the AI-assisted story, changes something that does not feel right, and explicitly approves the preview before entering checkout.

Category evidence: Gartner reported on May 27, 2026 that only 11% of surveyed U.S. consumers were willing to let AI make purchase decisions even in lower-stakes categories, while consumers showed more openness to AI tools that narrow choices. The design implication is assistance + human approval, not delegated purchase authority. This is category evidence, not PRINT MY STORY performance evidence.

Source: https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2026-05-27-gartner-survey-finds-consumers-want-ai-shopping-help-but-not-ai-purchase-decisions
