## 3) PRINT MY STORY AI

### Customer Truth

**Personalization should not require oversharing.**

The creation flow previously required an answer to each current prompt before advancing, including prompts about embarrassing moments and fears. RUN119 makes every prompt skippable and makes buyer control part of the product's marketing promise: personalization quality should come from the right specific details, not pressure to disclose more.

For adjacent child/family AI privacy context, a nationally representative SSRS survey of 1,004 parents for Common Sense Media reported that more than eight in ten parents were concerned about AI-enabled toys collecting children's personal information. This is not a personalized-book study and is not evidence of PRINT MY STORY demand; it reinforces why family-facing AI products should minimize pressure to disclose sensitive information.
Source: https://ssrs.com/news/how-parents-view-ai-enabled-toys-for-young-children/
