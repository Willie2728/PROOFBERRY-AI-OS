## 2. ProofBerry AI — Creative Strategy

### PB-DOC-019 — One-Role Design Partner Brief

Hook: **Don't start with an AI transformation. Start with one live role.**

The offer names:
- one active requisition and role-critical must-haves;
- one staffing-side owner;
- one client-side reviewer;
- the buyer's existing submission workflow retained for comparison;
- a proposed structured evidence-packet prototype;
- evidence provenance, coverage, gaps, conflicts, and unknowns;
- targeted follow-up only where review risk remains;
- a human-owned decision;
- an end-of-pilot expand / revise / stop decision.

Measurement dimensions are reviewer usefulness, recruiter effort, candidate friction, open questions, handoff clarity, and whether the workflow earns a second role. These are measurement dimensions, not promised outcomes.
