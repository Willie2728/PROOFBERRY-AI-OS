## 1. Customer Truth — ProofBerry

Fresh 2026 signal: job-search administration itself is becoming agentic.

Greenhouse reported on May 7, 2026 that, among more than 2,950 surveyed active job seekers, 30% said they were already using AI agents to search openings, submit applications, or schedule interviews.

Robert Half reported on March 10, 2026 that 65% of surveyed U.S. hiring managers said AI-enhanced applications made candidate skills harder to verify.

Run 12 thesis:

**Agentic application makes application administration more abundant. Candidate-owned, role-relevant evidence becomes more valuable.**

ProofBerry is not positioned against legitimate AI help. The wedge is what remains after search/application logistics become easier to automate: evidence a candidate can explain, demonstrate and stand behind, organized around what the role actually requires and reviewed by a human decision-maker.

## 4. Conversion / analytics readiness

Current first-party telemetry before the Run 12 measurement change contained 20 records:

- 18 `landing_view`
- 9 unique browser sessions represented by landing views
- 2 `waitlist_form_view`
- 0 `audience_select`
- 0 `waitlist_form_start`
- 0 `waitlist_submit`

All observed traffic was direct / organic / default and may include internal/test activity.

The repeated landing views within the same sessions exposed avoidable measurement noise. Run 12 implemented `PB-LP-003` in Base44:

- dedupe `landing_view` once per session + campaign + content variant after successful event creation;
- capture referrer host and hostname metadata;
- distinguish explicit audience selection from the form's default candidate state;
- record `waitlist_form_view` with audience unset until the visitor actually chooses a path.

This is measurement hygiene, not a claimed conversion improvement. The landing UX was otherwise left stable because the sample remains insufficient.
