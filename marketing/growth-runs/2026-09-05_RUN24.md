## 1. ProofBerry AI

### Customer Truth

Run 24 sharpened the staffing design-partner wedge around a market distinction that is becoming more important as recruiting platforms add stronger applicant evaluation and candidate-verification controls:

**An identity check is not a capability check.**

Current recruiting products are improving sourcing, shortlist creation, recruiter-defined applicant evaluation, prescreening, identity verification, and hiring-fraud controls. Those layers may help answer who entered the process and whether records or presence are authentic. The client still has a different question: what role-relevant evidence can I inspect, what remains uncertain, and what should I ask next?

ProofBerry should therefore position its early staffing design-partner motion as a capability-evidence layer beside the existing workflow, not as an identity-verification product unless that capability is separately implemented and verified.

Working handoff:

`role criterion → work artifact/demonstration → follow-up reasoning → recruiter reviewer note → open question → human decision owner`
