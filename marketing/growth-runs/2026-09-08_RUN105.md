## 2. VERLORAY AI Autonomous Business Generator OS

### Customer Truth

A founder considering paid membership should be able to define one governed mission before checkout: mission area, whether a human decision owner exists, which boundary must be verified, and the decision window. The readiness step should create value without becoming another mandatory checkout gate.

### Creative Strategy

**VER-TXT-008 — One Mission Before Membership**  
Hook: **Before you buy autonomy, write down the mission.**  
CTA: **Copy one no-contact mission-readiness brief before checkout.**

### Production Readiness

- `brief_copy`
- mission area
- human-owner-ready boolean
- boundary type
- decision window
- timestamp

No name, email, organization, free-text mission, or financial detail is stored in this signal.
