## 1. ProofBerry AI

### Customer Truth

**Proof only helps if qualified candidates stay in the process long enough to provide it.**

Run 30 adds an **evidence-burden budget** to the one-role staffing design-partner method.

Two 2026 field experiments support the design problem without proving a ProofBerry outcome:
- Jabarian & Henkel’s July 30 natural field experiment randomized roughly 70,000 applicants between a human recruiter and an AI voice interviewer; human recruiters still evaluated the interviews and made hiring decisions. The study reports more structured/consistent information collection and downstream improvements in that setting.
- A CESifo 2026 natural field experiment with more than 3,000 applicants reports that asynchronous interviews reduced application continuation by more than half in that setting, including among highly qualified applicants, with the largest deterrence among women.

Run 30 inference: **stronger evidence is not free if the collection method creates unnecessary candidate burden.**

Pilot method:
`role-critical criterion → lowest-burden useful evidence → gap / conflict / unknown → deeper follow-up only where decision risk remains → candidate-friction telemetry → human-owned decision`

Connected ProofBerry still contains **0 RubricCriterion records**, so this remains an illustrative early-access design-partner method rather than a finalized rubric, validated burden threshold, or selection model.

Research sources:
- https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28222
- https://www.ifo.de/en/cesifo/publications/2026/working-paper/brave-new-world-hiring-natural-field-experiment-how-asynchronous

## Growth decision

ProofBerry has enough unmeasured awareness inventory. The next high-value evidence remains **qualified, attributable recruiter/staffing traffic**.

Run 30 sharpens the buyer proposition: **do not maximize verification burden. Maximize decision-relevant evidence per unit of candidate effort.** Start with the few questions that materially affect one live role, use the lowest-burden useful evidence first, escalate only unresolved risk, measure candidate friction, and keep the consequential decision human-owned.
