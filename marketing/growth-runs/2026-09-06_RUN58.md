# 1. ProofBerry AI

## Customer Truth
A successful form submission should confirm the state the system actually created. It should not imply design-partner pilot acceptance, scheduling, access, a response-time SLA, or guaranteed follow-up when the verified submit path only records the request and emits a `waitlist_submit` event.

## Creative Strategy
**PB-TXT-068 — Request Recorded, Not Accepted**

Hook: **A submit confirmation should confirm the request — not invent an acceptance, SLA, or guaranteed follow-up.**

The commercial funnel remains deliberately separated:

1. `pilot_qualified` — the buyer has one live role, one accountable owner, a finite decision window, and a buyer-defined role #2 success rule.
2. `pilot_review_request_click` — the qualified buyer explicitly chooses to open the commercial-contact step.
3. `waitlist_form_start` — the buyer voluntarily begins entering contact details.
4. `waitlist_submit` — the request is recorded.

A `waitlist_submit` is not evidence that a pilot has been accepted, scheduled, assigned an SLA, started, or produced a staffing outcome.
