## 3) AvatarConnect

### Customer Truth
A selected likeness-authority option is not automatically a ready likeness. “Not ready yet” must remain an incomplete planning state, and a non-self likeness needs buyer-declared consent/authorization workflow readiness before contact unlocks.

### Creative Strategy
- **AVC-TXT-001** — “A selected likeness is not automatically a ready likeness.”
- **AVC-DOC-001** — Authority + Consent Fit Card.

### Production Readiness
`scopeReady` now accepts only `self`, `authorized_team_member`, or `licensed_talent` as ready authority states. For `authorized_team_member` and `licensed_talent`, `consent_workflow_ready` must also be true before contact becomes available. A self-likeness may proceed without the separate consent-workflow checkbox, while the use case, provider/runtime status, decision window, and evidence receipt remain required.
