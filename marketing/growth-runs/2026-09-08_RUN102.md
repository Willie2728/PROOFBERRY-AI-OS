## 3. KAMERON AIGENT SECURITY OS

### Customer Truth
A checkpoint proves that state can be captured. It does not by itself prove that a payment, email, ticket, database mutation, or other external side effect happens exactly once after recovery.

### Creative Strategy
**KAM-TXT-102 — Checkpoint ≠ Exactly-Once**

Hook: **A checkpoint proves state survived. It does not prove the payment, email, ticket, or write happened once.**

CTA: **Copy the bounded recovery proof brief before contact.**

### Production Readiness
Created Base44 entity `RecoveryScopeSignal` and added a no-contact **Bounded Recovery Proof Brief** to `src/pages/Pricing.jsx`.

The buyer can copy a reusable internal brief containing workflow, checkpoint state, side effects that must not repeat, required non-duplication receipt, resume authority, recovery objective, integration/runtime boundary or unresolved note, and decision window before sharing contact.

The anonymous `RecoveryScopeSignal` stores only:
- `event_type=brief_copy`
- decision window
- whether an integration boundary was supplied
- timestamp

It does not store workflow, checkpoint, side-effect, receipt, authority, company, or email text.
