# RUN135 — VerifyTrust: Explicit Assurance + Timing Before Contact

## Customer Truth
A default assurance value and optional timing field can make incomplete inbound interest look more commercially qualified than it is.

## Creative Strategy
- **VT-TXT-001** — “Choose the assurance target and decision window deliberately.”
- **VT-DOC-001** — Assurance + Timing Before Contact.

## Production Readiness
VerifyTrust now starts the assurance field blank and requires an explicit buyer choice: unsure/needs scoping, exploratory, IAL1, IAL2, or IAL3. The evaluation window is now a required deliberate selection: 0–30 days, 31–90 days, 90+ days, or exploring. Contact stays private until those choices plus the bounded workflow, provider boundary, privacy/retention requirement, and buyer-defined evaluation receipt are complete.
