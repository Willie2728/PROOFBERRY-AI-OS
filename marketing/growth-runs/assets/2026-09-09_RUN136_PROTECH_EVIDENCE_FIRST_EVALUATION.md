# ProTech OS — RUN136 Evidence-First Life-Safety Evaluation

## Customer Truth
A life-safety buyer should not be asked to trust unsupported delivery-speed, uptime, monitoring, rollout, partnership, or emergency-outcome claims. Qualified intent starts with one bounded environment and workflow plus the evidence the buyer expects to inspect.

## Creative Strategy
- **PTO-TXT-001** — “Define the evaluation before the demo.”
- **PTO-DOC-001** — Life-Safety Evaluation Proof Card.

## Production Readiness
The owned landing flow now requires a buyer to define a deployment environment, one bounded evaluation workflow, a decision window, and a required evidence receipt before contact fields appear. Those buyer-defined fields are persisted into the existing Lead record (`deployment_interest` plus structured notes) so contact alone is not mistaken for a qualified life-safety evaluation.

Unsupported or unverified homepage claims were replaced or bounded, including: `<3s` alert delivery, `99.99%` uptime SLA, `500+` facilities ready, `24/7` AI monitoring, “world’s most advanced,” instant/real-time/under-3-second workflow claims, proprietary drone fleet, staffed dispatch/first-responder routing, always-on social monitoring, and three named testimonial/case-study claims that had no supporting evidence in the connected sources. The testimonial area now presents buyer evaluation questions instead of social proof. Pricing is labeled proposed packaging and SLA/support terms are explicitly left to validation and contract.

The concept-film and gallery surfaces are now labeled as illustrative media rather than production/outcome evidence. The configured scene source contains 19 media URLs; their presence is not treated as evidence of production deployment or a real incident outcome.
