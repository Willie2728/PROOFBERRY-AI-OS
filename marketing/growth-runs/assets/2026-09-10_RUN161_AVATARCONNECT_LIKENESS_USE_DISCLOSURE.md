## Customer truth
An avatar buyer can possess or claim a likeness-authority basis while still leaving the actual use boundary undefined. A stronger qualified-intent funnel should distinguish who is authorized, where the synthetic likeness may appear, how it will be disclosed, how long the authority lasts, the provider/runtime state, and what receipt is required before a second workflow.

## Deployable owned copy
A likeness permission is not a blank check. Scope one use before you scale it. Define the authority basis, distribution context, synthetic-media disclosure plan, authority duration or revocability, provider/runtime status, and the first-workflow receipt. Contact comes after those boundaries are explicit.
