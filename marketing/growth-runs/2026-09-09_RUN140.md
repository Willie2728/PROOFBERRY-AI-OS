## 3. KAMVERAY AI OS

### Customer Truth
The one-project beta offered email/OTP and Google authentication, but verified growth attribution was implemented only for email/OTP. A Google path could therefore lose the one-project intent and UTM/creative context, biasing future conversion analysis by authentication method.

### Creative Strategy
- `KAMV-TXT-005`: **The sign-in method is not the product test.**
- `KAMV-DOC-004`: **Auth-Neutral Beta Receipt**.
- Product-test rule: one bounded, non-sensitive project must produce context useful enough to earn project #2; authentication is only the access path.
- Claim boundary: verified account creation is not proof of product use, retention, time saved, accuracy, security, ROI, willingness to pay, or production readiness.
