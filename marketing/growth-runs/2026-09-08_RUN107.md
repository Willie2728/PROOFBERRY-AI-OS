## 3. AI SWARMER OS

### Customer Truth

An enterprise security buyer may be able to define a useful control test before they are ready to share contact information. A bounded internal control-test brief is stronger qualified intent than an email alone.

### Creative / Product Change

Created **SWR-TXT-006 — Control-Test Brief Before Contact**.

Once the existing enterprise scope is complete, the Base44 UI now offers a no-contact **One-Workflow Control-Test Brief** before email. The brief carries the workflow, authority boundary, human approval actions or unresolved state, trust-expiry trigger, enforcement path, buyer-defined control receipt and decision window. It explicitly says that the brief is not pilot acceptance, security certification, deployment or evidence of protection.

RUN107 also created `EnterpriseScopeSignal` with `scope_ready` and successful `brief_copy` events. The signal stores only structured readiness booleans, decision window and attribution. It does not store company, workflow text, authority text, control-receipt text, buyer role or email.

NIST's 2026 work on software and AI agent identity and authorization emphasizes risks created when agents receive access to diverse data, tools and applications, and calls for identification, authorization, auditing and related controls. NIST's AI Agent Standards Initiative also highlights agent authentication, identity infrastructure and security evaluations. This supports SWARMER's bounded authority/control-receipt positioning, not a certification or conformance claim.
