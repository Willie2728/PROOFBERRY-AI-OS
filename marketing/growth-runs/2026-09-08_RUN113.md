## 2. ReelForge

### Customer Truth
A faster AI-assisted edit is not automatically a better creator workflow if the creator cannot preserve control over what the system changes. Reviewability, editability, undo/revert, change visibility, or a human publish/handoff approval can be part of the commercial pass criterion itself.

### Creative Strategy
Created **RF-TXT-006 — “Control is part of the pass criterion.”**

The no-contact Prototype Test Brief now requires one structured creator-control requirement:
- review before AI changes are applied;

- undo/revert AI-assisted changes;
