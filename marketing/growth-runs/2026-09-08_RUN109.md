## 3. Valore AI Security Sentinel

### Customer Truth
A museum, collector, insurer, conservator, or investor should be able to define one artifact context, one threat, and one proof receipt before giving contact information. A concept-stage architecture should not present thickness, conservation safety, satellite performance, tamper behavior, certification, theft prevention, recovery, or financing as completed facts without validation.

### Creative Strategy
- Asset: `VAL-SENT-TXT-001 — Make One Artwork Earn the Prototype Test`
- Hook: `Before you call it protection, define the receipt that would prove the test.`
- CTA: `Build the One-Artifact Threat Brief before sharing contact information.`

### Production Readiness
Active Base44 marketing was changed from assertive product certainty to a validation-first prototype posture:
- Hero labels the system as prototype architecture and treats thickness, off-grid link, and tamper detection as design/test targets.
- Problem section removes unsupported theft/recovery statistics and frames movement, tamper, telemetry/failure modes, and conservation approval as buyer evidence questions.
- Solution section treats low-visibility attachment, off-grid telemetry, edge event logic, and tamper evidence as prototype goals requiring testing.
- How It Works begins on sacrificial/conservator-approved test substrates, measures baseline/false positives, verifies event receipts, and measures alert handoff rather than promising instant global alerts.
- Investment copy labels the `$90K` prototype and `$1.2M` seed figures as planning estimates/targets, not delivered MVP or completed financing.
- The previous client-only email form could display success without durable persistence. It was replaced with a no-contact One-Artifact Threat Brief and an explicit technical-review-intent step.

New entities:
- `SentinelScopeSignal` — structured, privacy-minimized `brief_copy` / `review_request_click` events;
- `SentinelPilotRequest` — durable email + context/threat/window + buyer-defined proof requirement + attribution + timestamp + internal review status.

The success state now says only that a request was recorded for evaluation. It does not imply pilot acceptance, deployment, response time, conservation approval, certification, theft prevention, recovery, or commercial terms.
