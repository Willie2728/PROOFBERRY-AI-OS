## 1. Customer Truth — ProofBerry

Fresh 2026 evidence supports a trust problem on both sides of the hiring process:

- Greenhouse Candidate AI Interview Report, May 1, 2026: among 2,950 active job seekers surveyed, 63% had experienced an AI interview, 70% said they were not clearly told upfront that AI would evaluate them, and 38% said they had walked away from a hiring process because it included an AI interview. https://www.greenhouse.com/newsroom/63-of-job-seekers-have-faced-an-ai-interview-most-havent-had-a-good-one-yet
- Robert Half, March 10, 2026: 67% of surveyed U.S. HR leaders said reviewing AI-generated applications slowed hiring; 65% of hiring managers said AI-enhanced applications made candidate skills harder to verify. https://press.roberthalf.com/2026-03-10-Robert-Half-survey-67-of-HR-leaders-report-AI-generated-applications-are-slowing-hiring
- SHRM 2026 Talent Trends: nearly 70% of HR professionals reported challenges recruiting for full-time roles; skills gaps remain a major constraint. https://www.shrm.org/topics-tools/research/2026-talent-trends

Run 11 thesis: **Better hiring signal should not require treating every applicant like a fraud case.**

The live ProofBerry landing section `src/components/landing/ViralProofSection.jsx` was updated to replace a suspicion-heavy interview-cheating statistic with attributed candidate-transparency evidence and to move the public message toward role-relevant evidence, transparent evaluation, and human-owned consequential decisions.
