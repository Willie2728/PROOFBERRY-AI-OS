## 2) VerifyTrust

### Customer Truth
A default assurance value and optional timing field can make incomplete identity-proofing interest look more commercially qualified than it is.

### Creative Strategy
- **VT-TXT-001** — “Choose the assurance target and decision window deliberately.”
- **VT-DOC-001** — Assurance + Timing Before Contact.

### Production Readiness
The assurance field now starts blank and requires an explicit buyer choice: unsure/needs scoping, exploratory, IAL1, IAL2, or IAL3. The evaluation window is now a required deliberate selection: 0–30 days, 31–90 days, 90+ days, or exploring. Contact remains hidden until those choices plus the bounded workflow, provider boundary, privacy/retention requirement, and buyer-defined evaluation receipt are complete.
