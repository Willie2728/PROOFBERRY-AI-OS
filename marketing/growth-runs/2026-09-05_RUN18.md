- Greenhouse, June 10, 2026: applications were reported up 129% since 2023 while open roles stayed roughly flat; its new hiring features emphasize structured signal rather than automation without clarity.
- Robert Half, March 10, 2026: 65% of surveyed hiring managers said AI-enhanced resumes made skills harder to verify; 67% of surveyed HR leaders reported using staffing firms and 89% of those users said staffing partners were effective in addressing AI-related hiring challenges.
