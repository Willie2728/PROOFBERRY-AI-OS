## 3. PRINT MY STORY AI

### Customer Truth
A generated personalized-book preview is not automatically gift-ready, and a buyer who clicks Continue to Stripe is not automatically a paid customer. The product already had a useful three-signal gift-readiness self-check, but the analytics skipped the moment of checkout intent and only recorded successful checkout-session creation.

### Creative Strategy
Created **PMS-TXT-076 — Gift-Ready Signal**.

Hook: **AI can draft a personalized book. That doesn’t make it gift-ready.**

Buyer test: does it feel recognizably about them, is the tone right for the relationship, and is at least one detail specific rather than generic? Edit first; decide on checkout after.
