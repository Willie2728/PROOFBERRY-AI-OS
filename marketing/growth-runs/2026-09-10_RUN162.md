## Executive decision
RUN162 optimizes for qualified traffic by making three buyer trust boundaries explicit before contact or transaction intent:
1. ProofBerry: if AI touches evaluation, disclose its role, the candidate AI-use rule, human decision owner, evidence boundary, and question/exception path before assessment.
2. VerifyTrust: define the identity failure/abuse case before choosing a verifier, assurance target, or provider stack.
3. AURELIS: separate presented creator identity, AI use, provenance evidence, rights authority, edition/availability, and commercial terms rather than compressing them into an “original” claim.
