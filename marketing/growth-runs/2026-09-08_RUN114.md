## 2. Wilkerson AI University

### Customer Truth
AI-assisted task completion is not automatically a learning gain. The useful acquisition promise is therefore not “AI helps you finish faster”; it is **run a small task, then show yourself that you can explain, modify, or reproduce the result with less assistance**.

### Creative Strategy
- **WAU-TXT-001 — Learn It, Then Prove You Learned It**
  - Hook: **Finishing the AI-assisted task is not the learning receipt.**
  - CTA: build a no-contact Learning Receipt, then try the Coding Lab.

The receipt lets a visitor choose a learning goal and one proof step: explain it, change a variable and rerun it, or attempt the next step with less AI help.

## 3. MarketFlow AI

### Customer Truth
A campaign can hit a nominal conversion goal and still worsen the funnel if “qualified” is undefined. MarketFlow should require the operator to state **who counts as an in-scope prospect** before generating and scaling more creative.

### Creative Strategy
- **MF-TXT-002 — Define Who Qualifies Before You Scale**
  - Hook: **If you cannot define who qualifies, more leads can make the funnel worse.**
  - CTA: **Define who qualifies before you scale the creative.**

Updated sequence: `product context → qualified audience → qualified conversion goal → owned destination → channel hypothesis → creative angle → review → verified publishing handoff → attributable result`.

### Production Readiness
