## 1. ProofBerry AI / PAAOS

### Customer Truth
**Define what evidence earns review before the call.**

A recruiter can have a live requisition, accountable owner, finite decision window, buyer-defined role-2 success rule, and baseline yet still leave one evaluation question unresolved: what evidence must a candidate claim carry before it is ready for client or hiring-team review?

### Creative Strategy
**PB-TXT-108 — Evidence Rule Before Review**

Hook: **Define what evidence earns review before the call.**

CTA: Choose the minimum evidence packet a candidate claim must carry before client or hiring-team review, then request the one-role review.

The new rule is optional. RUN98 does not add another mandatory contact gate while ProofBerry still has no measurement-eligible production funnel events.

### Production Readiness
Base44 implementation adds a buyer-defined minimum evidence packet with four selectable requirements:
- reviewable work sample;
- context + candidate action;
- outcome / external receipt;
- reviewer trace / follow-up path.

Selected rules flow into the copied internal pilot brief, Pilot Fit Snapshot, durable WaitlistSignup message, and GrowthEvent metadata (`evidence_requirements`, `evidence_requirement_count`). They are explicitly buyer-defined requirements, not candidate verification, a credential, or a hiring recommendation.
