## 3. AURELIS

### Customer Truth
A configured policy, guide, loop name, or demo control is not proof that a particular artwork passed similarity, provenance, rights, or curatorial review. The Studio surface was overstating operational state: it described the loop as running around the clock and said every brief passed checks even though the current composer returns a curated catalog result in demo mode.

Provenance context: OpenAI's current provenance guidance says signals such as Content Credentials and SynthID can help indicate origin, but they are not guarantees that content is accurate, unedited, legally owned, or correctly contextualized. This supports separating origin/review signals from commercial-rights claims.
Source: https://help.openai.com/en/articles/8912793-c2pa-in-dall-e-3

### Creative Strategy
Created **AUR-DOC-006 — Generation Review State Key**, with the rule: **A policy is not a passed review.** The card separates demo result, intended workflow, evidenced reviewed state, and commercial readiness. A reviewed state requires an item-level durable run/review receipt; commercial readiness additionally requires verified availability, edition/terms, rights or licensing authority where applicable, payment/provider state, and fulfillment path.

The Studio now says the current composer is a demo, changes the CTA from `Generate original` to `Preview demo result`, changes loading language to `Preparing demo`, and removes the unsupported claim that every brief already passes similarity + rights checks.
