# ProofBerry RUN157 — AI-Assistance Boundary

## Customer Truth
AI use is increasingly normal in real work and increasingly ambiguous in hiring assessments. A useful evaluation should not rely on an unstated assumption about whether AI assistance is permitted. It should declare the rule before interpreting the evidence.

## Creative
**PB-TXT-167:** “If AI is part of the job, say where AI is allowed in the evaluation.”

## Buyer enablement
**PB-DOC-073 — AI-Assistance Boundary Card**

Before a one-role review, define:
1. the job-relevant task;
2. whether AI assistance is allowed, allowed with disclosure, restricted, prohibited, or still undefined;
3. the inspectable evidence the reviewer expects;
4. the accountable human reviewer and exception rule; and
5. the distinction between a declared assessment policy and any claim that undisclosed AI use can be reliably detected.
