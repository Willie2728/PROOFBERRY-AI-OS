# RUN152 — ProofBerry AI: AI-Use Evidence Receipt

## Customer Truth
AI capability is becoming a job requirement in more non-software roles, while AI-assisted applications make self-reported skill harder to interpret. The commercial opportunity is not to punish AI use; it is to make role-relevant contribution inspectable.

Current verified ProofBerry baseline at run start: 0 measurement-eligible production GrowthEvents. No Winner Library promotion is justified from that baseline.

External signals reviewed:
- Financial Times, 2026-09-06: UBS is adding AI proficiency to junior-banker hiring requirements beginning in 2027.
- Business Insider, 2026-09-06: employers are encountering hidden prompt-injection text in some resumes, illustrating how AI-mediated screening can create new trust problems.

These are category signals only. They do not establish ProofBerry performance, accuracy, or hiring outcomes.

## Creative Strategy
**PB-TXT-162**

Hook: **Don't ask only whether they used AI. Ask what they can show you after using it.**

Qualified CTA: **Bring one live role and define the AI-assisted work sample, human contribution trace, inspectable artifact, outcome receipt, and human review owner that would count before interpreting the claim.**

Supporting buyer-enablement asset: **PB-DOC-068 — AI-Use Evidence Receipt**.

The receipt asks the buyer to define:
1. one job-relevant task;
2. the candidate's contribution;
3. the AI/tool role;
4. an inspectable artifact;
5. an observable outcome or validation method;
6. a human review owner.

Confidential prompts, secrets, customer data, and proprietary material are explicitly outside the requested evidence boundary.
