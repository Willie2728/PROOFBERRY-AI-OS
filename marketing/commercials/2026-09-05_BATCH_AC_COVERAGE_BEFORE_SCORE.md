## PB-TXT-039 — LinkedIn buyer copy

Most candidate summaries answer: **what is the score?**

A staffing client may need a more useful question: **what evidence do we actually have for each role criterion — and what is still unresolved?**

For one live role, ProofBerry’s early-access pilot method can organize the handoff around:
- role criterion
- evidence present
- gap / conflict / unknown
- next review question

Then the recruiter or client still owns the consequential decision.

The goal is not to make a bigger AI claim. It is to make the review surface clearer.
