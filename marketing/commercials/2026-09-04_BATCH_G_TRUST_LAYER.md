**Faster screening does not solve weak signal. It only processes weak signal faster.**

The hiring stack is getting more automated on both sides. Candidates can produce stronger applications faster. Recruiting teams can process more applications faster.

But speed does not answer the decision question: can this person do the work?

ProofBerry AI is being built around a different layer — structured, role-relevant capability evidence that can be inspected by hiring teams and used by AI-assisted systems without handing the hiring decision to a black box.
