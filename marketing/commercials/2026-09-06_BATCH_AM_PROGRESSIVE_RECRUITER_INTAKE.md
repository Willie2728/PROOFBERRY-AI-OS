# PB-TXT-049 — Progressive Recruiter Intake

**Audience:** Staffing agency owners, recruiting leaders, client-facing recruiters, pilot owners  
**Channel:** LinkedIn, founder outreach, sales enablement  
**Status:** Production-ready text only. Not published.

## Hook

**One form should not make an explorer pretend to be a pilot.**

## Post

A staffing firm can be interested in ProofBerry without having a live role ready today.

Those are two different buyer states, so the intake should treat them differently.

If you **have a live requisition**, use the one-role path and add the three signals that make a design-partner review measurable:

- the active role,
- the person who owns the pilot decision,
- and a finite 14-, 30-, or 60-day window for deciding whether the workflow earns role #2.

If you are **still exploring**, join staffing early access without filling out a pretend pilot application.

The point is not to inflate a lead score. It is to preserve the difference between curiosity and a test we can actually learn from.

ProofBerry is being structured as an early-access, human-governed evidence workflow. The consequential hiring decision remains with the recruiter or client reviewer.

## CTA

**Have a live role? Choose the live-role path. Still exploring? Join staffing early access without pretending you are pilot-ready.**

## Claims boundary

Do not convert this buyer-qualification method into claims of validated hiring outcomes, identity or fraud verification, legal compliance, bias elimination, predictive validity, ATS integration, placement improvement, ROI, or automatic hiring.
