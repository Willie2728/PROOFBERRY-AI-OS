# PB-TXT-054 — Ask the buying question first

**Product:** ProofBerry AI

**Audience:** staffing agency owners, recruiting leaders, pilot owners

**Customer Truth:** A qualified staffing buyer should be able to declare buyer state before being asked for contact details.

**Hook:** Ask the buying question before the contact question.

## Deployable copy

If a staffing buyer lands on ProofBerry, the first useful thing to learn is not the email address. It is whether the buyer is exploring or has a live role.

The recruiter path now puts that choice before contact details. Exploring stays lightweight. A live-role selection can reveal the optional requisition, accountable owner, and finite decision-window fields.

That classifies the buyer-side test; it does not score the candidate. Consequential hiring decisions remain human-owned.

**CTA:** Have one live role? Choose the live-role path, preview the proposed evidence packet, and add an owner plus finite decision window if you have them.
