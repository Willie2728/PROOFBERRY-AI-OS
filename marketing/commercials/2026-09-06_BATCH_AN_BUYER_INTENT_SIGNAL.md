## Customer truth
A staffing buyer can be curious about ProofBerry without having a live requisition ready to test. Those are different commercial states and should be measured separately.

Bullhorn's 2026 GRID Industry Trends Report surveyed nearly 2,300 recruitment professionals. It reports that only 10% of firms have AI embedded throughout their workflow and that 20% say they lack a clear implementation plan. That supports a staged buyer-intent path; it does **not** prove ProofBerry conversion performance.

Source: https://www.bullhorn.com/grid/2026-industry-trends/report/

## Creative
**Hook:** The first qualified signal is not an email address. It is: “I have a live role.”

A staffing team can be interested in ProofBerry without being ready to run a pilot today. So the intake should preserve that difference instead of forcing every recruiter into the same form.

If you are exploring, join staffing early access with a lightweight path.

If you have a live requisition, choose the live-role path. Then add the pilot owner and a finite decision window if you have them.

That does not score the candidate. It tells us whether there is a measurable buyer-side test worth discussing.

One role. One accountable owner. One decision window. Consequential hiring decisions remain human-owned.

**CTA:** Have a live role? Choose the recruiter path and select “I have a live role to test.” Still exploring? Join early access without pretending you are pilot-ready.
