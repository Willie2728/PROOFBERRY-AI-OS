## PB-TXT-036 — LinkedIn buyer post

**Hook:** The safest place to start with AI in hiring may be information collection — not the hiring decision.

A 2026 field experiment involving roughly 70,000 applicants found that AI voice interviews could collect hiring-relevant information more consistently in that setting while human recruiters still evaluated the interviews and made the hiring decisions.

That is a useful design principle for a staffing pilot, not a promise of identical results everywhere:

**standardize the evidence collection → keep the consequential judgment human**

For one live role, define the capability that matters, ask the same core evidence question, capture the candidate's work or explanation, record what remains uncertain, and hand that packet to a recruiter or client reviewer who owns the decision.

ProofBerry's design-partner wedge should be that narrow: not an AI verdict, not a rip-and-replace, and not a mystery score.

One role. A clearer evidence protocol. Human-owned decisions.

CTA: **Bring one live role and test the collection protocol beside the workflow you already use.**
