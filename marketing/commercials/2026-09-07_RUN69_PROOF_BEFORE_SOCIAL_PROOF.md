# WCL Growth Run 69 — ProofBerry AI

## Customer Truth
ProofBerry should not use anonymous beta praise, star ratings, or unattributed outcome anecdotes to establish trust in a product whose value proposition is evidence quality. Buyer confidence should come from an inspectable one-role review boundary and attributable category research.

## Creative Strategy
**PB-TXT-079 — Proof should come before social proof.**

Hook: If your proof product needs anonymous praise to prove itself, the proof surface is backwards.

CTA: Bring one live role and inspect the evidence boundary before you request the one-role review.

Copy:
Proof should come before social proof.

A staffing buyer should be able to inspect the proposed one-role workflow before being asked to trust anonymous praise. ProofBerry now leads with the bounded test: one live role, one accountable owner, one finite decision window, evidence provenance, unresolved questions, and a human-owned hiring decision.

The category problem is real, but the research is not a ProofBerry result. Robert Half reported on March 10, 2026 that 67% of surveyed U.S. HR leaders said AI-generated applications were slowing hiring and 65% of hiring managers said AI-enhanced resumes made candidate skills harder to verify.

Bring one live role. Inspect what the review would show. Then decide whether the one-role conversation is worth having.
