# ProofBerry AI — Run 56 — Validate the Scope Before the Call

## Customer Truth
A qualified B2B buyer may want to complete most evaluation independently and involve a seller later for validation, risk reduction, and decision support. ProofBerry should therefore make the bounded one-role review easy to inspect before asking a buyer to start a commercial conversation.

## Creative Strategy
**Hook:** The best sales conversation starts after the buyer can verify what the pilot does — and what it does not do.

**Body:** B2B buyers increasingly want to research on their own, then bring a seller in when they need validation and risk reduction. ProofBerry’s staffing path is being designed around that behavior. A qualified buyer can review the proposed evidence packet, inspect the exact one-role review scope, carry an internal pilot brief, and only then choose whether to request the conversation. The scope card is explicit about what the review can cover and what it does not promise. The goal is not more calls. It is fewer, better conversations with buyers who understand the bounded test before they share contact details.

**CTA:** Validate the one-role scope first. Then request the conversation only if the bounded test fits.
