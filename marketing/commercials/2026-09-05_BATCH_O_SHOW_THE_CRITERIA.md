## PB-TXT-023 — Recruiter LinkedIn

Status: **Production ready — not published**

Before an AI-assisted assessment, four questions should be answerable: What role criterion are we evaluating? What evidence counts? What will AI do? Who owns the decision?

Greenhouse reported in 2026 that 70% of U.S. candidates who experienced AI evaluation were not clearly told upfront that AI would evaluate them. SHRM has separately emphasized validating whether hiring tools measure what they are supposed to measure and giving candidates comparable opportunities to demonstrate capability.

ProofBerry is being built around a more inspectable handoff: **role criterion → candidate evidence → human review.**

Show the criteria before the score.

## PB-TXT-024 — Candidate LinkedIn / X

Status: **Production ready — not published**

If AI is part of the evaluation, you should know what is being evaluated.

A transparent hiring process can tell you the role criterion, ask you to show relevant evidence, explain where AI assists, and keep a human accountable for the decision.

ProofBerry is being built to make capability evidence more inspectable and role-relevant — not to reduce a person to a mystery score.

**Show the criteria. Show the work. Keep the decision human.**
