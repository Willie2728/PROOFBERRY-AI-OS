## Buyer proposition

**Proof should not become an obstacle course.**

A staffing firm does not need every applicant to complete the deepest possible verification flow. It needs enough role-relevant evidence to resolve the questions that materially affect the client decision — without creating unnecessary candidate friction.

## PB-TXT-040 — LinkedIn buyer post

More verification is not automatically more useful hiring signal if the process drives qualified people out before review.

A 2026 CESifo field experiment with more than 3,000 applicants found that asynchronous interviews reduced application continuation by more than half in that setting, including among highly qualified applicants.

That does not mean avoid structured evidence. It means candidate burden should be a design variable.

For one ProofBerry staffing pilot:
- identify the few role-critical questions;
- ask for the lowest-burden useful evidence first;
- escalate only unresolved high-risk questions;
- track starts, completions, abandonment, burden feedback, and reviewer usefulness.

The goal is stronger evidence without turning proof into an obstacle course. The recruiter or client still owns the consequential decision.

**CTA:** Bring one live role and set the evidence-burden budget before the pilot starts.

## Claims boundary

The CESifo finding must remain attributed to that field experiment. Do not generalize it into a ProofBerry outcome. Do not claim a validated assessment-burden threshold, compliance, bias elimination, predictive validity, placement improvement, ATS integration, ROI, or automatic hiring.
