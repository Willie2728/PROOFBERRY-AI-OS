## PB-TXT-032 — Keep Your Stack

Status: **production-ready text; not published**.

Most staffing firms do not need another rip-and-replace AI pitch.

Bullhorn's 2026 GRID report says only 10% of staffing firms have AI embedded throughout their workflow. That makes the first ProofBerry question smaller and more useful:

**Can one live role produce a clearer client review when a candidate evidence packet sits beside the submission process you already use?**

Keep your ATS. Keep your normal packet. Keep the recruiter in control.

For one role, add:
- the role criterion,
- candidate work evidence,
- follow-up reasoning,
- a recruiter reviewer note,
- and the open question the client still needs answered.

Then compare the review.

Did the client understand the candidate more clearly? Was the added recruiter effort reasonable? Was candidate friction acceptable? Did the workflow earn a second role?

If yes, expand. If not, stop.

ProofBerry is in early access and is not presented as an automatic hiring system or a guaranteed ATS integration.
