## Customer Truth
A recruiter can self-educate through the Recruiter Evidence Review Kit and still disappear into an unattributed scoping path. Better growth measurement should preserve the buyer-enablement source without adding another form field or treating a click as a qualified lead.

## Creative Strategy
**PB-TXT-122**

Hook: **If your hiring process can’t explain what evidence a human reviewed, another AI score won’t fix it.**

Body: AI hiring tools can make screening faster. That does not make a hiring decision explainable. Before you add another score, ask three questions: What exact evidence can a hiring manager inspect? Who owns the consequential decision? What rule decides whether one live role deserves a deeper review? ProofBerry’s Recruiter Evidence Review Kit is built for that pre-contact step. No promise of a hiring result, compliance outcome, or pilot acceptance—just a clearer way to decide whether the workflow is worth scoping.

CTA: **Open the Recruiter Evidence Review Kit. Bring one live role, one evidence rule, and one accountable human owner before you scope the conversation.**
