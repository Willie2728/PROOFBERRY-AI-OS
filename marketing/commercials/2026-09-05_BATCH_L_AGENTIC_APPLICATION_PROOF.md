# ProofBerry Batch L — Agentic Application / Candidate-Owned Proof

## Campaign idea

**Let the agent handle the admin. Keep the proof candidate-owned.**

This family responds to the rise of AI agents handling job-search logistics without framing legitimate AI assistance as cheating. ProofBerry is positioned around the evidence a candidate can explain, demonstrate and stand behind, with consequential hiring decisions remaining human-owned.

That changes the bottleneck. Faster applications do not answer the hiring question: what can this person actually do?

ProofBerry is being built to organize candidate-owned, role-relevant evidence that a human hiring team can inspect, discuss, and weigh.

Let the agent handle the admin. Keep the proof inspectable and the decision human.
