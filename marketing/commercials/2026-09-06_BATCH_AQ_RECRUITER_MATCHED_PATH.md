## Customer Truth

Qualified recruiter traffic should not land on candidate-first copy. A staffing buyer arriving on a recruiter-qualified URL should immediately understand the one-role design-partner path, see the proposed evidence packet, and be able to distinguish exploratory interest from a live-role test.

## Hook

**Qualified recruiter traffic should land on recruiter copy.**

## Deployable copy

If I send a staffing leader to ProofBerry, the first screen should not sound like candidate marketing.

The recruiter path now starts with one live role, a proposed evidence packet, and a clear choice: **“I’m exploring”** or **“I have a live role to test.”**

The consequential hiring decision stays human-owned.

If you have one active requisition, preview the proposed one-role evidence packet before you decide whether a pilot conversation is worth your time.

## CTA

**Have one live requisition? Preview the proposed packet, then choose the live-role path.**
