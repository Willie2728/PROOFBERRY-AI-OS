## PB-DOC-005 — One Role Pilot Blueprint

Hook: **Pilot one live role. Not your whole stack.**

Format: 1200×1600 SVG
Audience: staffing agency owners, recruiting leaders, client-facing recruiters
Use: LinkedIn document/image, sales follow-up, ProofBerry recruiter waitlist support
CTA: Request staffing design-partner early access.

Sequence:
1. Choose one live role with a named recruiter and human decision owner.
2. Agree a small set of role-relevant evidence questions.
3. Run a small candidate cohort.
4. Build the recruiter-to-client handoff: criterion → evidence → recruiter note → follow-up question.
5. Decide whether the workflow earned a second role.

Guardrails printed on the asset: no automatic hiring, no guaranteed placement speed, no bias-elimination claim, no predictive-validity claim; human reviewers keep consequential decisions.

## PB-TXT-028 — Staffing Buyer LinkedIn

**You do not have to replace your recruiting stack to test whether better evidence improves the client handoff.**

Staffing firms are already automating more of the repetitive work around sourcing, screening, outreach, and submittal preparation. That makes the human handoff more valuable, not less.

ProofBerry is being built to test a narrower question: can a recruiter give the client a more inspectable evidence packet for one live role without replacing the recruiting stack?

Start with one role. Agree the evidence questions. Run a small candidate cohort. Review the handoff. Then decide whether the workflow earned a second role.

Human reviewers keep the decision.

CTA: **Request a one-role ProofBerry design-partner pilot.**
