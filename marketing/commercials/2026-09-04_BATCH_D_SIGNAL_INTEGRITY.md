## PB-TXT-003 — Recruiter Thought Leadership
Audience: recruiters / TA leaders
Channel: LinkedIn
Format: text post

Application quality is getting harder to infer from polish alone.

Candidates have better writing tools. Recruiters have better screening tools. Both sides are using AI — and that means hiring systems need a stronger layer of evidence between "this person says they can" and "we understand why we should move them forward."

That is the problem ProofBerry is being built around.

Not replacing recruiter judgment. Not pretending a score can make the hiring decision. Structuring role-relevant demonstrations, work evidence, follow-up evaluation, and capability signals so a human decision has more to work with than keywords and presentation quality.

AI can help a candidate present themselves. The opportunity is helping them prove themselves.
