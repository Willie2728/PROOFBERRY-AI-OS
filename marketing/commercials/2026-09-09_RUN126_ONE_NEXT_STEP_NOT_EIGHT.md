# PB-TXT-136 — One Next Step, Not Eight Equal CTAs

## Customer Truth
A qualified buyer can still stall when every buyer-enablement resource is presented as an equal next action. ProofBerry already has useful evidence, fit, retention, handoff, and review aids; RUN126 stops making the buyer choose among all of them at once.

## Hook
**One qualified next step beats eight equal CTAs.**

## Deployable copy
If the one-role review is still missing evaluation-quality boundaries, ProofBerry now recommends the Review Quality Scorecard. If the buyer has defined all four evaluation-quality signals, ProofBerry recommends the Qualified Review Handoff Card. The rest of the buyer aids remain available as optional deeper review.

## CTA
Take the one recommended next step for the current review state, then request the one-role review only when the buyer actually wants a commercial conversation.

## Claims boundary
This is funnel guidance, not proof of higher conversion, reduced time-to-decision, pilot acceptance, candidate verification, hiring performance, or revenue. RUN126 does not claim a lift without attributable production telemetry.
