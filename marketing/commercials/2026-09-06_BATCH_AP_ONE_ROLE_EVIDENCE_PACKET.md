## Deployable copy
A staffing pilot should not end with “the AI ran.” It should end with something a recruiter and client reviewer can inspect.

For ProofBerry’s proposed one-role design-partner path, that means a packet that keeps the role requirements beside the candidate-provided evidence, surfaces gaps or conflicts, records targeted follow-up, preserves reviewer notes, and ends with an explicit **expand / revise / stop** decision about role #2.

The packet informs the reviewer; it does not make the consequential hiring decision.

If you have one live requisition, one accountable owner, and a finite decision window, preview the proposed packet and request a one-role review.

## CTA
Have one live role? Preview the proposed evidence packet, then request a one-role design-partner review.

## Claims boundary
This is a proposed early-access pilot method. It is not evidence of validated selection, identity verification, fraud detection, predictive validity, bias elimination, ATS integration, placement improvement, ROI, legal compliance, or automatic hiring. Consequential hiring decisions remain human-owned.
