## PB-TXT-016 — Recruiter / workforce post

**Hook:** Better hiring signal should not require treating every applicant like a fraud case.

Recruiting teams need stronger signal, but suspicion-first hiring creates a second problem: candidate trust. Greenhouse reported in May 2026 that 63% of surveyed active job seekers had faced an AI interview, 70% were not clearly told upfront that AI would evaluate them, and 38% said they had walked away from a process because it used an AI interview.

ProofBerry is being built around a different standard: define the role-relevant evidence, tell candidates what is being evaluated, let them explain and demonstrate the work, and keep consequential decisions human-owned. Better proof should improve trust on both sides.

## PB-TXT-017 — Candidate post

**Hook:** You should not have to choose between being evaluated by AI and being treated fairly.

AI is already part of hiring. The better question is whether you know what is being evaluated, whether the evidence actually relates to the role, and whether a person remains accountable for the decision.

ProofBerry is being built to help candidates turn capability into inspectable, role-relevant proof without pretending one score can define a person. Explain it. Do it. Show it. Keep the human decision-maker in the loop.
