# PB-TXT-059 — One-Click Success Rule

## Hook
Qualification should be specific without making the buyer write an essay.

## Copy
A qualified staffing pilot needs a success rule, but the buyer should not have to write an essay to create one.

ProofBerry now offers fast buyer-defined starting points for whether a one-role pilot earns role #2:

- Easier client review
- Less recruiter rework
- Clearer evidence gaps
- Fewer unresolved questions
- Other / custom

The buyer still owns the standard for whether role #2 is a yes. The selection describes the staffing-side pilot objective; it is not a candidate score and does not make the consequential hiring decision.

## CTA
Bring one live role, one accountable owner, one finite decision window, then choose the success rule that would make role #2 worth expanding to.
