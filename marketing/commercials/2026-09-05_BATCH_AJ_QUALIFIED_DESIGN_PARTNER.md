## PB-TXT-046

**Hook:** A waitlist lead is not the same thing as a design partner.

A waitlist lead is not the same thing as a design partner. For a one-role ProofBerry staffing pilot, three buyer signals matter before we call the opportunity qualified: there is an active requisition, someone owns the pilot, and there is a decision window for whether the workflow earns a second role.

Those signals do not guarantee a successful pilot. They tell us whether there is a measurable test worth running.

Keep the current submission workflow beside it. Compare the client handoff. Track recruiter effort, candidate friction, unresolved questions, and reviewer usefulness. Keep the consequential hiring decision human-owned.

**CTA:** If you have one live role, one pilot owner, and a decision window, request staffing design-partner access.
