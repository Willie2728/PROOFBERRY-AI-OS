## PB-TXT-022 — Recruiter LinkedIn post

**Status:** Production-ready text only. Not published.

**Hook:** Before the live interview, give the recruiter a review packet — not another black-box score.

**Copy:**

Recruiting teams are being asked to move faster while protecting candidate experience and human judgment.

The useful handoff before a live interview is not another opaque score. It is a review packet: the role criterion, the evidence observed, what remains uncertain, and the follow-up question a human should ask next.

ProofBerry is being built around that evidence brief. AI can help organize and summarize the signal. The hiring team keeps the judgment.

**CTA:** Join ProofBerry early access to preview the evidence-brief workflow.

## Creative strategy

This batch deliberately moves down-funnel. Rather than generating another broad awareness carousel, it demonstrates the product artifact a recruiter could actually use in a hiring workflow. The primary conversion hypothesis is that concrete decision-support will attract more qualified recruiter/employer interest than abstract "better signal" language.
