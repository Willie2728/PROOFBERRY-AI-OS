# RUN106 — PB-TXT-116: 60-Second Evidence Check

## Customer Truth
A recruiter may agree with evidence-first hiring before they are ready to scope a live role. The next useful action should therefore create qualified intent without demanding contact information or implying that a pilot has started.

## Creative Strategy
**Hook:** Can your hiring manager audit the claim in 60 seconds?

**Body:** Before you scope a live role review, run the One-Role Evidence Review Checklist. Put the capability claim, reviewable work sample, context and candidate action, outcome or external receipt when available, reviewer trace, and unresolved evidence into one inspectable handoff.

**CTA:** Open the One-Role Evidence Review Checklist before scoping a live role.

**Claims boundary:** The checklist is a scoping aid. It is not skill verification, a credential, a hiring recommendation, pilot acceptance, or evidence of a hiring outcome.
