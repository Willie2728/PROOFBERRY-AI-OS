## PB-TXT-041 — LinkedIn buyer post

Most verification workflows have a hidden cost: when something looks uncertain, they add another test for everyone.

That can create more candidate burden without making the client handoff more useful.

A narrower staffing workflow is possible:

1. Capture what the candidate states.
2. Capture what a relevant work sample, interview, or reviewer actually shows.
3. If those signals align, stop adding burden.
4. If they conflict or remain unclear, ask one targeted follow-up tied to the contradiction.
5. Preserve the unresolved question for the recruiter or client reviewer who owns the decision.

The important boundary: a contradiction is not a fraud verdict. It is a reason to look closer at one specific question.

SHRM's 2026 assessment-design guidance discusses performance discrepancies and follow-up explanation as useful signals when AI can help candidates fake skills in real time. That does not prove a ProofBerry outcome. It does support a better design question:

**Can we collect less evidence overall by escalating only the evidence that does not agree?**

ProofBerry is building toward that kind of inspectable, human-owned review workflow.

**Design-partner CTA:** test one live role beside your current process. Keep your stack, keep the hiring decision human, and compare whether the client handoff becomes easier to inspect.
