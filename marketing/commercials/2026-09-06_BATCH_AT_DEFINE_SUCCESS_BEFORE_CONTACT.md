# ProofBerry AI — PB-TXT-057

## Customer Truth
A staffing pilot is not decision-ready just because the buyer has a live role, an owner, and a deadline. Before asking for contact details, the buyer should also be able to say what would make role #2 a yes.

## Creative Strategy
**Hook:** The pilot is not qualified until the buyer defines what success would look like.

**Body:**
One live requisition gives ProofBerry something real to test. One accountable owner gives the pilot someone who can make the decision. A finite 14-, 30-, or 60-day window prevents endless experimentation. But there is still one question to answer before the contact form: **what would make role #2 a yes?**

That success criterion can be practical: easier client review, less recruiter rework, clearer evidence gaps, or a more useful handoff. It belongs to the buyer-side pilot. It is not a candidate score and it does not make a hiring decision.

**CTA:** Bring one live role, one owner, one decision window, and one buyer-defined success criterion.
