## PB-DOC-015 — Evidence Provenance Card

**Hook:** A verification label is not enough. Show where the evidence came from.

Flow:
1. Candidate-stated — claim, explanation, credential or experience described by the candidate.
2. Demonstrated — work sample, role exercise, answer or observable demonstration.
3. Recruiter-observed — reviewer context, clarification or follow-up.
4. Third-party check — only when an actual external check exists and is named precisely.
5. Unresolved — preserve the open question instead of hiding it behind a score.
6. Client review → human-owned decision.

**CTA:** Bring one live role and label the evidence before you score it.

## PB-TXT-038 — LinkedIn buyer post

“Verified” can hide too much.

For a staffing client, the useful questions are: **verified how, by whom, and against what?**

A one-role ProofBerry design-partner pilot can label each signal as candidate-stated, demonstrated, recruiter-observed, third-party checked when available, or unresolved — then preserve the supporting artifact and the open question for human review.

The goal is not to replace the recruiter’s judgment. It is to make the handoff easier to inspect.

Bring one live role and compare the evidence-labeled packet beside the workflow you already use.
