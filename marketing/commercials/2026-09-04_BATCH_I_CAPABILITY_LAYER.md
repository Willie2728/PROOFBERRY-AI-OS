Slide 2: Identity asks: “Is this person who they say?” That is an important trust layer. But it is not the whole hiring question. Next comes capability.

## PB-TXT-012 — Recruiter thought leadership
**Status:** PRODUCTION READY
**Channel:** LinkedIn
**Hook:** Identity verification can tell you who is applying. It cannot, by itself, tell you who can do the job.

Hiring trust is becoming a stack. One layer helps establish identity. Another helps organize and match applications. The next question is still human and practical: what can this person actually do?

ProofBerry is being built around structured, role-relevant evidence — explanations, demonstrations and inspectable work — so a recruiter can evaluate more than document polish.

Better evidence. Human-owned decisions.

## PB-TXT-013 — Candidate thought leadership
**Status:** PRODUCTION READY
**Channels:** LinkedIn, X
**Hook:** Being verified as a real person is important. Being able to show what you can do is different.

Hiring is adding more trust checks: identity, fraud signals, AI-assisted matching and structured screening. For candidates, that makes one thing more valuable — role-relevant evidence you can explain and stand behind.

ProofBerry is being built to help turn “I can do this” into something a human reviewer can inspect.

Explain it. Do it. Show it.

## Claims guardrails
Do not say ProofBerry replaces identity verification, detects fraud, guarantees interviews, guarantees job placement, or makes hiring decisions. Keep all capability claims framed as what the product is being built to support unless the feature is verified live.
