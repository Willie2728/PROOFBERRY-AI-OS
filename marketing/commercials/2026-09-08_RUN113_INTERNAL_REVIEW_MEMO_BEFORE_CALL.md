# RUN113 · ProofBerry AI · Internal Review Memo Before the Call

## Customer Truth
A recruiter can understand evidence-first hiring and still be unable to advance a vendor conversation internally. The missing object is often not another claim or form field; it is a compact memo that tells hiring, legal, security, procurement, or management what is being reviewed, what evidence counts, who owns the human decision, what baseline applies, and what unresolved issue should stop the test.

## Creative Strategy
**PB-TXT-123 — “Make the internal case before the sales call.”**

CTA: build the no-contact **PB-DOC-037 One-Role Internal Review Memo**, then scope a live role only when the evidence rule, accountable human owner, decision window, comparison baseline, and unresolved risks are explicit.

## Production Readiness
PB-DOC-037 is a browser-local memo builder. It does not submit form data. Empty fields become **Unresolved** instead of assumptions. The memo covers:
- live role;
- accountable human decision owner;
- capability claim to inspect;
- minimum evidence rule;
- AI/human decision boundary;
- comparison baseline;
- decision window; and
- stop/escalation rule.

The Recruiter Evidence Review Kit (PB-DOC-036) now includes the memo as Step 4 before a human scoping conversation. The PB-DOC-037 CTA carries owned buyer-enablement attribution into the recruiter scoping path.
