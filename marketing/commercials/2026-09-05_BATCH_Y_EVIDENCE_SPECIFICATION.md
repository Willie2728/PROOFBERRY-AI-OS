## PB-TXT-035 — Define the Evidence Before You Score It

**Hook:** Skills-first hiring gets vague fast if nobody defines what counts as evidence.

**Production-ready copy:**

Skills-first hiring gets vague fast if nobody defines what counts as evidence.

Before a staffing pilot scores, summarizes, or ranks anything, the client should be able to answer five questions: What capability matters for this role? What evidence is relevant? What should a human ask when the evidence is incomplete? What did the recruiter observe — including uncertainty? And who owns the consequential decision?

That is the ProofBerry design-partner wedge: not another generic skills list, and not an automatic hiring promise. One live role. One evidence specification. One reviewable packet beside the workflow the staffing firm already uses.

**CTA:** Bring one live role and define the evidence before the pilot starts.

**Status:** production-ready text only. Not published.
