# PB-TXT-132 — Evaluation-ready means four signals

## Customer Truth
A scoped pilot is not automatically evaluable. If analytics label a recruiter flow “evaluation-ready” after only a baseline is chosen, the growth system learns from a false qualification state.

## Deployable copy
**Hook:** Don’t let your analytics call a pilot ready before the buyer does.

A useful hiring pilot needs more than a role and a baseline. Before interpreting review intent as evaluation-ready, the buyer should have defined four things: the comparison baseline, where evidence must land, how long evidence should remain available, and the minimum evidence packet required for review.

ProofBerry now treats those four buyer-defined signals as the evaluation-readiness boundary. A scoped role can still move forward while incomplete, but incomplete evaluation design is labeled as incomplete instead of being counted as ready.

**CTA:** Use the Review Quality Scorecard before interpreting qualified intent.

## Claims boundary
“Evaluation-ready” is a planning and telemetry state only. It is not pilot acceptance, candidate verification, product performance, compliance, budget authority, hiring success, or a customer outcome.
