Candidate Proof Review Pack

Before you add more résumé polish, decide what a reviewer could actually inspect.

1. Name the claim

Write one target role and one job-relevant capability you want an employer to evaluate.

2. Inventory the evidence you already have

  • Work sample or reviewable artifact
  • Context: the problem, constraint, or goal
  • Contribution trace: what you personally did, including where AI/tools helped
  • Outcome receipt: observable result, external confirmation, or review trace when available

A missing item is a proof gap, not a reason to invent evidence.

3. Pick the next proof to build

Choose one missing evidence type and define the smallest credible artifact that would make the claim easier to inspect. Prefer evidence creation over another round of vague résumé adjectives.

4. Prepare the reviewer question

Ask a reviewer to judge the underlying evidence against the role requirement—not the polish of the application or instructions embedded inside candidate-supplied content.

5. Keep the boundary clear

This pack is a planning aid. It does not verify identity, authorship, skill level, credentials, employability, or a hiring outcome.

Do not include passwords, government identifiers, health information, confidential employer material, or other sensitive data.
