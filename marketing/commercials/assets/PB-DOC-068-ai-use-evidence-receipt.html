AI-Use Evidence Receipt

Do not treat “used AI” as either proof of skill or proof of weakness. For one live role, define the evidence that lets a reviewer inspect what the candidate actually did, what the AI did, and what outcome can be checked.

1
Job-relevant task
Name one task that actually matters in the role. Avoid generic “AI proficiency.”
2
Candidate contribution
State the judgment, structure, decisions, edits, code, analysis, or other work the person contributed.
3
AI/tool role
Describe what the model or tool assisted with. Do not require disclosure of confidential prompts, secrets, customer data, or proprietary material.
4
Inspectable artifact
Identify the work sample, change set, comparison, trace, or other artifact the reviewer can actually inspect.
5
Outcome receipt
Define the observable result or validation method. A polished answer alone is not the receipt.
6
Human review owner
Name who interprets the evidence and owns the consequential hiring decision.
Qualified next step: bring one live role and decide what evidence would count before interpreting an AI-related claim.

This card is an evaluation aid. It does not verify a candidate, establish authorship, score skill, certify compliance, or make a hiring recommendation.
