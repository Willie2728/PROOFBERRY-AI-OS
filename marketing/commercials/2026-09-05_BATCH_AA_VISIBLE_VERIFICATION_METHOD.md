# ProofBerry Batch AA — Visible Verification Method

## PB-DOC-014 — Make the Verification Method Visible

Hook: **Don’t just say “we verify candidates.” Show the method.**

Pilot method:
1. Name the role signal.
2. Collect comparable evidence.
3. Record uncertainty and the follow-up question.
4. Add recruiter context.
5. Hand the evidence chain to a human client reviewer.

CTA: **Bring one live role and compare a visible evidence method beside the workflow you already use.**

## PB-TXT-037 — Your Verification Process Is Part of the Product

Your verification process is part of the product you sell to the client.

Staffing firms are being asked to do more than move candidates quickly. Clients increasingly want confidence that the person in the submission can actually demonstrate what the role requires. Robert Half reported in March 2026 that 65% of surveyed hiring managers said AI-enhanced resumes made skills harder to verify, while 89% of HR leaders using staffing firms said those partners were effective at addressing AI-related hiring challenges.

That makes the verification method itself part of the staffing offer.

ProofBerry’s design-partner wedge should make the method inspectable:

`name the role signal → collect comparable evidence → record uncertainty → add recruiter context → hand the evidence chain to the client reviewer who owns the decision`

Don’t ask the client to trust a black box. Show the method for one live role beside the workflow you already use.

CTA: **Bring one live role and compare the client handoff with the verification method made visible.**
