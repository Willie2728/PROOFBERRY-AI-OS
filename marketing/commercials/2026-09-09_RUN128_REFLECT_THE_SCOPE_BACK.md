# RUN128 — ProofBerry: Reflect the Scope Back Before Asking for More Time

## Customer Truth
A qualified buyer should not have to reconstruct the scope they already submitted when ProofBerry follows up. The first commercial outreach should reflect the buyer-defined role/workflow and evaluation boundaries back to them before asking for a meeting.

## Creative Strategy
**PB-TXT-138 — Reflect the Scope Back.**

The admin follow-up draft now includes the buyer-submitted bounded scope when available: live role or workflow boundary, accountable owner, decision window, success rule, evaluation baseline, and review-quality state. The message asks the buyer to correct anything inaccurate or incomplete before a conversation is treated as worthwhile.

## Deployable copy
> Thanks for requesting a ProofBerry one-role or one-workflow scoping review. I am reflecting the scope back before we ask for more of your time. This message does not mean the review, pilot, deployment, or scheduling has been accepted yet.
>

>
> Please reply if any of the scope above is wrong or incomplete. If it is accurate, we can use it to decide whether a bounded review is worth a conversation.
