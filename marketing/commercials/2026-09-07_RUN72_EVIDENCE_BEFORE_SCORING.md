# RUN72 — ProofBerry AI — Evidence Before Scoring

## Customer Truth
A hiring score is useful only when the buyer can inspect what produced it. Interview completion, a model score, a human review, and a hiring decision are separate facts.

## Creative Strategy
**Hook:** A hiring score is useful only when you can inspect what produced it.

**Deployable copy:**
A number can summarize evidence. It cannot replace evidence. ProofBerry’s application flow now records interview completion without manufacturing a score, strengths, weaknesses, key moments, or an automatic hiring recommendation when no verified scorer produced them. Historical model scores can remain visible as context, but the recruiter is directed back to the recording, transcript, challenge artifacts, provenance, and unresolved questions before deciding the next step.

**CTA:** Bring one live role. Inspect the evidence before the score—and the score before the decision.
