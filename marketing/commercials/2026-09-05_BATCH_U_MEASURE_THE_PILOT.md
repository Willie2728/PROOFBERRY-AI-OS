## PB-TXT-031 — Recruiter / staffing LinkedIn

**Before you pilot another AI hiring tool, decide how you will judge the pilot.**

SHRM's 2026 State of AI in HR report found that 56% of surveyed HR professionals said their organizations do not formally measure the success of AI investments.

For a ProofBerry staffing design-partner pilot, the question should not be "Did we use AI?" It should be narrower:

Did the client get a clearer evidence handoff? Did the recruiter spend less or more effort explaining the submission? Did the workflow help the client ask better follow-up questions? Did it create unreasonable candidate friction? And, at the end of one live role, did the workflow earn a second role?

That is the pilot.

Not a rip-and-replace. Not an automatic-hiring promise. One role, explicit success criteria, human-owned decisions, then an evidence-based expansion decision.
