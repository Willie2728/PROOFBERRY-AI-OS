## PB-TXT-047 — Three blanks

**Hook:** Three blanks can tell you whether you have a pilot or just interest.

A recruiter email alone is not a design-partner signal.

For the one-role ProofBerry early-access path, fill three blanks:

- Which requisition is live?
- Who owns the pilot decision?
- When will you decide whether this earns role #2?

**0 / 3** = general interest.  
**1–2 / 3** = partial readiness.  
**3 / 3** = a measurable design-partner review.

None of this changes candidate scoring or hiring decisions. It classifies the buyer’s readiness to run a bounded comparison.

Keep the current submission workflow beside it. Measure reviewer usefulness, recruiter effort, candidate friction, unresolved questions, and whether the process earns another role. Keep the consequential hiring decision human-owned.

**CTA:** Bring one live role, one owner, and one decision window.
