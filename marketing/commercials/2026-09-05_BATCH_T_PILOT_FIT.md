# ProofBerry Batch T — Pilot Fit

## Customer truth
Staffing platforms are automating more screening, verification, and submittal preparation, while buyers still need trustworthy, human-governed review. Current market evidence supports a low-friction ProofBerry wedge: qualify one live role first instead of asking a staffing firm to replace its stack.

Sources reviewed 2026-09-05:
- Robert Half, 2026-03-10: 65% of surveyed hiring managers said AI-enhanced resumes made skills harder to verify; 67% of HR leaders use staffing firms and 89% of those users said those partners were effective with AI-related hiring challenges.
- Bullhorn, 2026-05-28: Amplify Digital Workers automate staffing tasks including verification and workflow execution.
- Greenhouse Real Talent, current 2026 product page: identity verification, fraud signals and AI matching remain distinct from employer-owned hiring decisions.

## Creative strategy
**Thesis:** The next buyer objection is not “what is ProofBerry?” but “is this worth piloting in my workflow?”

**PB-DOC-007 — Staffing Pilot Fit Card**
Hook: **Is one live role a good pilot fit?**
Buyer qualification:
1. One live role with clear client expectations.
2. A real need to make candidate capability easier to inspect.
3. A small candidate cohort the recruiter can review normally.
4. Willingness to compare today's handoff with an evidence packet.
5. A human recruiter/client retains the consequential decision.
6. Permission to learn from workflow behavior without relying on sensitive identity data.

**PB-TXT-030 — Recruiter LinkedIn**
Staffing firms do not need another rip-and-replace software conversation.

A useful ProofBerry pilot can start much smaller: one live role, one existing client workflow, and a small candidate cohort.

The question is not whether AI can produce another score. The question is whether the client handoff becomes easier to review when role criteria, candidate evidence, follow-up reasoning, recruiter notes, open questions, and human decision ownership are organized together.

If that workflow does not earn a second role, stop there.

ProofBerry is inviting staffing and recruiting teams to help test the one-role design-partner model.

CTA: Request staffing design-partner access.
