**Buyer enablement:** PB-DOC-077 — **AI Workflow Evidence Test**

## Customer truth
AI proficiency is becoming an explicit hiring requirement outside traditional software roles, but a self-reported AI-skill claim does not tell a recruiter what the candidate can actually do in a job-relevant workflow. The conversion surface should therefore help a buyer predeclare the task, allowed assistance, evidence packet, reviewer question, and decision rule before interpreting candidate evidence.

## Deployable owned copy
If AI skill matters to the role, test the workflow—not the résumé wording. Pick one job-relevant task. Declare where AI is allowed. Require an inspectable evidence packet. Then ask one human reviewer question the evidence should be able to answer.

**CTA:** Open the AI Workflow Evidence Test and define the role, workflow, AI-assistance rule, minimum evidence packet, human reviewer question, and pass / needs-more-evidence rule before candidates begin.
