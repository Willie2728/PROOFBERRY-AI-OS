# ProofBerry AI / SYNTHSAPIENS — RUN89

## Asset
**PB-TXT-099 — Workflow Before Contact**

**Hook:** Don’t send your email before you can name the workflow.

A workforce catalog can help you imagine the role. It cannot prove the operating boundary.

For a serious SYNTHSAPIEN or AI-workforce test, start with one bounded workflow: what the system would do, what still requires human approval, and what receipt would prove the work actually happened.

ProofBerry now keeps contact fields hidden on the employer/workforce path until the buyer names an organization, defines one workflow, and explicitly requests a scoping review.

**CTA:** Define one bounded workflow. Then request a one-workflow scoping review.

## Customer Truth
Qualified workforce demand is more useful when the buyer can state the real operating workflow before surrendering contact information. A catalog view, scoping click, or form submission is not deployment evidence.

## Product / Conversion Change
RUN89 updates the employer/workforce acquisition path so contact remains private until an organization and bounded workflow are present and the buyer explicitly requests review. Future `employer_workforce_early_access` requests are now surfaced through the same operator continuity controls used for qualified staffing requests: commercial-fit classification, follow-up state, owner, next action, due date, and aging attention.

## Evidence Boundary
This asset and workflow do not claim that a SYNTHSAPIEN is deployed, that an integration is production-ready, that a pilot is accepted, or that any autonomous execution occurred. Expansion should require an execution/output receipt and a human-owned decision boundary.
