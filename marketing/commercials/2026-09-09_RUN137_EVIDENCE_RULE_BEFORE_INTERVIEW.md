# RUN137 — ProofBerry AI
## PB-TXT-147 / PB-DOC-053 — Evidence Rule Before Interview

### Customer Truth
AI-assisted applications can increase the amount of polished candidate material without making job-relevant capability easier to inspect. A hiring team gets a cleaner evaluation when it defines the role-level evidence rule before the first candidate shapes the standard.

### Creative Strategy
**Hook:** Write the evidence rule before the interview changes your mind.

**Deployable copy:**
> AI can make an application more polished. That does not make the underlying skill easier to inspect. Before your next interview, write the evidence rule for the role: what capability matters, what minimum evidence packet you would accept, where that evidence must land, and which decision stays human-owned. Then apply the same rule to every candidate. ProofBerry is testing that evidence-first workflow with one live role at a time. Bring one role; define the packet before the person.

**CTA:** Bring one live role and define the minimum evidence packet before requesting a one-role review.
