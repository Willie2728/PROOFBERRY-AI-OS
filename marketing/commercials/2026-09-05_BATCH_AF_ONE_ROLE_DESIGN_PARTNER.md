## PB-DOC-019 — One-Role Design Partner Brief

**Hook:** Don't start with an AI transformation. Start with one live role.

**Audience:** staffing agency owners, recruiting leaders, client-facing recruiters, pilot owners

**Format:** 1200×1600 static card

**Offer:** A bounded early-access staffing pilot that keeps the existing submission workflow in place for comparison.

### Buyer brings
- one active requisition and role-critical must-haves;
- one staffing-side owner;
- one client-side reviewer;
- the existing submission process as the control/reference workflow.

### Proposed pilot deliverable
- a structured evidence-packet prototype for the selected role;
- evidence source labels plus coverage, gaps, conflicts, and unknowns;
- targeted follow-up prompts only where review risk remains;
- review notes that keep the consequential decision human-owned;
- an end-of-pilot expand / revise / stop decision.

### Measurement dimensions
Reviewer usefulness, recruiter effort, candidate friction, open questions, handoff clarity, and whether the workflow earns a second role. These are pilot measurement dimensions, not promised outcomes.

**CTA:** Bring one live role. Compare the handoff.

## PB-TXT-042 — Design-Partner Outreach

**Hook:** If your staffing firm is curious about AI but does not want a rip-and-replace project, start with one role and keep the current workflow beside it.

**Copy:**

If your staffing firm is curious about AI but does not want a rip-and-replace project, start smaller.

One live requisition. One staffing-side owner. One client reviewer. Keep the submission process you already trust.

ProofBerry's early-access design-partner pilot is being structured around a simple comparison: can a role-specific evidence packet make the client handoff easier to inspect without turning the system into the hiring decision-maker?

The proposed packet labels where evidence came from, what role criteria it addresses, what remains missing or conflicting, and which follow-up question is actually worth asking next.

Then measure the things that matter before expanding: reviewer usefulness, recruiter effort, candidate friction, unanswered questions, and whether the process earns a second role.

No transformation promise. No automatic hiring claim. One bounded test with a human-owned decision.

**CTA:** If you have one active requisition that would make a useful design-partner pilot, use it as the comparison case.
