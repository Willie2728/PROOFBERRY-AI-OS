# RUN162 — ProofBerry Candidate AI Evaluation Notice

## Customer Truth
AI now touches both sides of hiring. Qualified employers need a clearer pre-assessment contract: where AI may assist, what remains a human decision, what candidate AI use is allowed, what evidence will be inspected, and where questions or exceptions go. The conversion goal is a better-scoped live-role conversation, not higher form volume.

## Creative Strategy
**PB-TXT-172** — “If AI touches the evaluation, tell the candidate what it does—and what it does not decide.”

The recruiter early-access surface now frames transparency and human accountability before evidence collection rather than treating AI proficiency as a generic claim.

## Buyer Enablement
**PB-DOC-078 — Candidate AI Evaluation Notice**

The notice asks the buyer to predeclare:
- one live role and job-relevant task;
- where AI may assist;
- which consequential decision remains human-owned;
- the candidate AI-assistance rule;
- requested evidence and sensitive-data boundaries;
- accountable human reviewer;
- candidate question, exception, or accommodation path.
