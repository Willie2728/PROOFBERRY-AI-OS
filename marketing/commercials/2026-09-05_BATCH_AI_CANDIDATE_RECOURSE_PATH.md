## PB-TXT-045 — Buyer post

A disclosure without a human question path is only half a trust contract.

If a staffing pilot tells a candidate where AI is used, the next question should be just as clear: who can the candidate reach if the process is confusing, evidence is misunderstood, or an unusual access or support need appears?

For a one-role ProofBerry design-partner pilot, write that path before evidence collection starts: disclose the AI-assisted step, name the human contact or channel, preserve candidate clarification, route exceptions to an accountable reviewer, and record what remains unresolved. The consequential hiring decision stays human-owned.

The goal is not to promise a universal legal right or an automatic score change. It is to make the process more inspectable for the candidate and the staffing buyer.
