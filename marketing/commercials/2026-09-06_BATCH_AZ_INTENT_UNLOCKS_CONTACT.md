# PB-TXT-065 — Intent Unlocks Contact

## Customer Truth
A qualified pilot is not a sales conversation until the buyer explicitly chooses to start one.

## Hook
**A qualified pilot is not a sales conversation until the buyer explicitly opens the contact step.**

## Deployable copy
Most pilot forms ask for the email first and figure out fit later. ProofBerry reverses that order.

A staffing buyer first defines one live role, one accountable owner, one decision window, and what would make role #2 worth expanding to. Only then does the buyer choose whether to request the one-role review. Contact stays private until that explicit step.

The goal is not more form fills. It is a cleaner signal that a qualified buyer actually wants the conversation.

## CTA
Define one bounded role. Qualify the pilot. Then choose **Request the one-role review** to unlock contact.

## Claims boundary
This is buyer-side qualification and commercial-intent messaging. It is not a hiring recommendation, candidate score, validated selection result, fraud/identity claim, ROI claim, or evidence of production deployment.

## Distribution state
Production-ready text only. Do not mark Scheduled, Posted, published, or live until an authenticated channel action is verified.
