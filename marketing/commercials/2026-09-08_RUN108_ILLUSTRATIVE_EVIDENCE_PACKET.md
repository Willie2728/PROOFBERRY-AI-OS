# ProofBerry RUN108 — Illustrative Evidence Packet

## Customer Truth

Recruiters do not need another abstract promise that evidence is better than résumé language. They need to see what an inspectable evidence packet would actually contain before they are asked to scope a call. Because ProofBerry still has no measurement-eligible production denominator, RUN108 adds no mandatory form field.

## Creative Strategy

**PB-TXT-118 — The résumé is the claim. What would the receipt actually show?**

CTA: **See an illustrative evidence packet, then run the one-role checklist before scoping a review.**

Paired static decision aid: **PB-DOC-034 — Illustrative Evidence Packet**.

The hypothetical Customer Success Manager example shows four evidence categories:
1. reviewable work sample / artifact;
2. context plus the action the candidate says they personally took;
3. outcome or external receipt when legitimately available;
4. reviewer trace and unresolved follow-up.

The page explicitly keeps missing evidence unresolved rather than converting unknowns into “verified.” It is not a candidate record.

Market context: Robert Half reported on March 10, 2026 that 67% of surveyed U.S. HR leaders said AI-generated applications were slowing hiring and 65% of hiring managers said AI-enhanced résumés made skills harder to verify. That supports showing buyers what inspectable evidence means; it is not ProofBerry performance evidence.
Source: https://press.roberthalf.com/2026-03-10-Robert-Half-survey-67-of-HR-leaders-report-AI-generated-applications-are-slowing-hiring
