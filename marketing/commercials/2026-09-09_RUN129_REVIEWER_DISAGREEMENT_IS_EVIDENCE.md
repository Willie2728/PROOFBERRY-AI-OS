## Customer Truth
When two reviewers interpret the same candidate evidence differently, hiding that disagreement behind one score destroys useful decision context.

## Creative Strategy
**Hook:** Reviewer disagreement is evidence, not noise.

When two reviewers interpret the same candidate evidence differently, do not average the disagreement away. Preserve the criterion, the exact evidence each person inspected, both interpretations, the missing evidence that could resolve the difference, and the accountable human decision. Agreement is not proof of correctness, and disagreement is not a failed candidate.

**CTA:** Use the Reviewer Disagreement Receipt when two reviewers interpret the same evidence differently.
