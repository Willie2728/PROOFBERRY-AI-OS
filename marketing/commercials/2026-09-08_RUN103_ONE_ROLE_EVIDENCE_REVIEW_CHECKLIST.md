# RUN103 — ProofBerry One-Role Evidence Review Checklist

## Customer Truth
A qualified buyer request is more useful when the evidence rule can survive the internal recruiter → hiring-manager handoff. RUN103 therefore adds a reusable decision aid instead of adding another mandatory contact field.

## Creative Strategy
**PB-TXT-113 — Can a hiring manager audit the claim in 60 seconds?**

Before a one-role review earns a sales call, make the evidence portable. Name the live role. Name the capability claim. Define the minimum packet: a reviewable work sample, context and candidate action, an outcome or external receipt when available, and a reviewer trace. Name the human decision owner, the comparison baseline, the decision window, and the rule for whether a second-role test is worth considering.

Then protect the handoff: internal owner, next verified action, due time.

**CTA:** Run one live role through the evidence-review checklist before the call.
