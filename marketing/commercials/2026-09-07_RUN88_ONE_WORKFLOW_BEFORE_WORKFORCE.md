# RUN88 — PB-TXT-098 — One Workflow Before Workforce

## Customer Truth
A workforce catalog can describe roles, but it cannot prove that a role is production-ready inside a specific employer environment. Serious buyers need one bounded workflow, a named human approval boundary, known integrations, and a verifiable execution or output receipt before expanding.

## Creative Strategy
**Hook:** Do not buy an AI workforce from a catalog. Prove one bounded workflow first.

**Core copy:** Your catalog can show the role. It cannot prove the deployment. Start with one bounded workflow. Name the human approval boundary. Verify the integrations. Require an execution or output receipt. Expand only after the first workflow earns it.

**CTA:** Choose one SYNTHSAPIEN role, define the human approval boundary, and verify the integration and output receipt before expanding.
