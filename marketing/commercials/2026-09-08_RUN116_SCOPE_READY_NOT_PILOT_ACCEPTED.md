# RUN116 — PB-TXT-126 — Scope-Ready Is Not Pilot-Accepted

## Customer Truth
A completed self-reported intake form is not a qualified pilot. It is a scope-ready buyer proposal until ProofBerry independently verifies commercial fit, authority, and acceptance.

## Creative Strategy
**Hook:** A filled-out form is not a qualified pilot. A clear scope is.

**Primary copy:**
Define one live role, one accountable owner, a finite decision window, and what would make role #2 a yes. ProofBerry can use that to understand the proposed review. But form completeness does not mean the pilot is accepted, budget is approved, authority is verified, or product fit has been established.

**CTA:** Define the one-role scope, then explicitly request review.

## Production Readiness
The owned ProofBerry recruiter flow now labels the state `scope_ready` rather than `design_partner_ready`, emits `pilot_scope_ready` rather than treating form completion as `pilot_qualified`, and tells buyers that the state is not pilot acceptance or verified commercial qualification. Final Base44 build passed.

## Claims Boundary
`scope_ready` means only that buyer-provided scoping fields are complete. It is not pilot acceptance, budget verification, authority verification, a sales-qualified opportunity, a hiring result, or product-performance evidence.
