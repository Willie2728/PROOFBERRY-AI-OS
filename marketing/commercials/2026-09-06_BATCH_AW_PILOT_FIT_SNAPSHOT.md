# PB-TXT-060 — Pilot Fit Snapshot

**Run:** WCL Growth Run 50  
**Product:** ProofBerry AI  
**Audience:** Staffing and recruiting buyers  
**State:** Production-ready text only. Not scheduled. Not published.

## Customer Truth

A qualified staffing buyer should receive something useful before being asked for contact information. The next conversion step should reward qualification, not merely harvest an email address.

## Hook

**A qualified buyer should get a fit snapshot before giving you their email.**

## Deployable copy

Most staffing funnels ask for contact information first. ProofBerry is testing the opposite.

A recruiter can define one live requisition, the accountable pilot owner, a finite 14/30/60-day decision window, and the buyer's own rule for whether the workflow earns role #2. ProofBerry then shows a Pilot Fit Snapshot before the contact step.

The point is not more emails. It is a smaller number of buyer-qualified design-partner conversations with a reviewable success rule.

Current category research from Bullhorn shows staffing firms are adopting AI unevenly across full workflows, with data readiness, security, and unclear implementation strategy still limiting broader adoption. That is market context only, not evidence of ProofBerry performance.

ProofBerry's one-role path is designed as a bounded evaluation with human-owned consequential hiring decisions. It is not an autonomous hiring verdict.

**CTA:** Bring one live role, one owner, one decision window, and define what would make role #2 a yes.
