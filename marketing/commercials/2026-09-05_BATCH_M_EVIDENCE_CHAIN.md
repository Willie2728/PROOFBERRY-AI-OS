## PB-TXT-020 — Recruiter / workforce LinkedIn

If a hiring score cannot point back to the role criterion and the evidence that produced it, the number is doing too much work.

AI can be useful for organizing candidate information, structuring assessments and helping teams review larger volumes. But the review becomes stronger when a hiring manager can ask three simple questions:

What role criterion are we evaluating?

What did the candidate actually say, do or produce?

What evidence can a human inspect before making the decision?

That is the direction ProofBerry is being built around: role-relevant evidence that is easier to discuss, compare and review without pretending an AI score is the hiring decision.

**Role criteria → candidate evidence → human review.**

## PB-TXT-021 — Candidate LinkedIn / X

A score should never be the end of your story. The evidence behind it should be inspectable.

In the AI-assisted hiring era, polished applications and automated scoring are becoming easier to produce. That makes something else more valuable: being able to explain the reasoning, do role-relevant work and show the evidence behind your ability.

ProofBerry is being built around that idea — not replacing the human decision, but helping make the work behind your capability easier to inspect.

**Explain it. Do it. Show it. Let the evidence travel with the conversation.**
