# ProofBerry Batch AL — A Pilot Needs a Decision Window

Date: 2026-09-06

Status: Production-ready text only. Not published.
Audience: Staffing agency owners, recruiting leaders, pilot owners
Channel: LinkedIn / founder outreach / sales enablement

## Hook

**Curiosity is not a pilot. A pilot has a decision window.**

## Copy

A staffing team can be interested in AI and still not be ready to evaluate it.

For ProofBerry's one-role design-partner path, three buyer-side signals matter:

- one active requisition;
- one named pilot owner;
- one finite decision window for whether the workflow earns role #2.

"Exploring" is still welcome. It just should not be counted as the same thing as a measurable pilot.

That distinction matters because the point of early access is not to manufacture activity. It is to create a bounded comparison that can end with **expand, revise, or stop**.

The consequential hiring decision remains human-owned. This readiness check classifies the buyer's ability to run a pilot; it does not score candidates or promise hiring outcomes.

## CTA

Bring one live role, one owner, and a 14-, 30-, or 60-day decision window.

## Claims boundary

Do not claim validated selection performance, compliance certification, identity verification, fraud detection, bias elimination, predictive validity, ATS integration, placement improvement, ROI, or automatic hiring.
