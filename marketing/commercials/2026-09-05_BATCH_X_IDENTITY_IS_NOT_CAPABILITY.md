## PB-DOC-011 — Identity Is Not Capability Card

Hook: **An identity check is not a capability check.**

Purpose: buyer enablement for staffing design-partner outreach. Separate the identity/fraud layer from the role-relevant capability-evidence layer without attacking either category.

Flow:
1. Identity/fraud layer: who entered the process, presence, record consistency.
2. Capability evidence layer: role criterion, candidate work artifact/demonstration, follow-up reasoning, recruiter reviewer note, open question.
3. Human decision: the packet supports judgment; it does not replace it.

## PB-TXT-034 — LinkedIn buyer copy

Identity verification can answer one hiring question. It cannot answer the role question by itself.

Recruiting platforms are getting better at sourcing, screening, identity checks, fraud controls, and shortlist creation. Those are useful layers.

They still do not answer the narrower client question by themselves: **What role-relevant evidence can I inspect, what remains uncertain, and what should I ask next?**

ProofBerry's staffing design-partner wedge is an evidence-enhanced handoff beside the workflow you already use:

`role criterion → candidate work artifact or demonstration → follow-up reasoning → recruiter reviewer note → open question → human decision owner`

Identity controls can help establish who entered the process. The evidence packet should help the client judge what the person can do.

Start with one live role and compare the handoff.

CTA: **Bring one live role where résumé polish is not enough and compare the existing client handoff with an evidence-enhanced one.**
