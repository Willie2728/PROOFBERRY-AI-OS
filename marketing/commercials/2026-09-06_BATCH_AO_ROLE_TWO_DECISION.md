Date: 2026-09-06

## Customer truth

A pilot is only useful when it ends in a decision. For the current ProofBerry staffing design-partner path, the useful expansion question is not whether the team generated activity; it is whether one live-role comparison produced enough decision-quality evidence to expand, revise, or stop.

Robert Half reported on March 10, 2026 that 67% of surveyed U.S. HR leaders said AI-generated applications were slowing hiring and 65% of hiring managers said AI-enhanced resumes made skills harder to verify. That is category evidence for a bounded staffing-side evaluation, not ProofBerry performance evidence.

Source: https://press.roberthalf.com/2026-03-10-Robert-Half-survey-67-of-HR-leaders-report-AI-generated-applications-are-slowing-hiring

## Hook

**The pilot is not the proof. The role #2 decision is.**

## Deployable copy

If a staffing team tests ProofBerry on one live requisition, the useful question is not whether the team generated activity.

It is whether the client handoff became easier to inspect, what happened to recruiter effort, where candidate friction appeared, what questions remained unresolved, and whether the workflow earned a second role.

Keep the current submission workflow as the baseline. Review the one-role test with the staffing owner and client reviewer. Record one of three decisions: **expand, revise, or stop — and why.**

Consequential hiring decisions remain human-owned.

## CTA

Have a live requisition? Bring one role, one accountable owner, and a 14-, 30-, or 60-day decision window.
