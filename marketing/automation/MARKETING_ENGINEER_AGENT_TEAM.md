# PROOFBERRY Marketing Engineer Agent Team

Inspired by the Growth OS / Marketing Engineer model: marketing is treated as a system that continuously gathers truth, produces creative, distributes, measures, evaluates, and improves.

## Team
- **PB-MKT-01 — Marketing Director / Growth Orchestrator**: owns waitlist KPI, backlog, prioritization, daily brief and agent assignments.
- **PB-MKT-02 — Customer Truth Agent**: mines candidate/recruiter/employer language, objections, pain points, market evidence and competitive positioning.
- **PB-MKT-03 — Creative Strategist**: produces hooks, concepts, scripts, VSL variants and reusable creative recipes.
- **PB-MKT-04 — Production Agent**: generates Remotion/Base44/agent-media production briefs, shot lists, captions, thumbnails and cutdowns.
- **PB-MKT-05 — Distribution Agent**: prepares channel-native TikTok, Reels, Shorts, LinkedIn and X packages and posting queues.
- **PB-MKT-06 — Experiment & Analytics Agent**: tracks hook, retention, CTR, landing conversion and waitlist conversion; declares winners/losers with evidence.
- **PB-MKT-07 — Marketing Engineer**: maintains automations, analytics events, landing variants, content schemas, integrations and the growth cockpit.
- **PB-MKT-08 — Librarian / Content Vault Agent**: tags, deduplicates, versions, archives and promotes winners into reusable recipes.
- **PB-MKT-09 — Compliance / Claims Reviewer**: blocks fabricated results, testimonials, guarantees, misleading comparisons and unsafe outbound.

## Orgo/OpenClaw persistent loop
1. Read product source-of-truth + latest analytics.
2. Customer Truth agent updates insights.
3. Generate prioritized hypotheses by audience/channel.
4. Create batches of creative variants using winning recipes + controlled new ingredients.
5. Render/prepare channel packages.
6. Queue approved distribution through connected posting systems.
7. Capture metrics after publishing.
8. Evaluate against prior variants.
9. Promote winning hooks/structures/visual recipes into `winning-patterns/`.
10. Generate next batch from evidence and repeat.

The system should remain persistent on the always-on Orgo/OpenClaw environment, but posting requires valid connected channel credentials and must respect platform limits and anti-spam rules.

## Primary metric
Qualified ProofBerry waitlist signups.

Secondary metrics
Landing conversion rate; qualified traffic; video retention; completion rate; CTR; saves/shares/comments; recruiter/employer interest; requests for pilot/customer conversations.

## Initial production target
Maintain a ready-to-deploy inventory rather than waiting for requests: 30 short-form concepts, 10 rendered-ready scripts, 5 recruiter/employer pieces, 5 candidate education pieces, 3 VSL variants, 3 landing-page hero variants, and a rolling backlog of 20 experiments.
