## Go-live gate

An asset can move from `production_ready` to `queued` only when:
1. Render exists and is visually reviewed.
2. Burned-in captions are verified.
3. Claims Reviewer approves copy.
4. CTA resolves and contains asset-specific UTM parameters.
5. Publishing connector or authorized external posting path is verified.

An asset can move from `queued` to `published` only when a platform post ID/URL is captured.
