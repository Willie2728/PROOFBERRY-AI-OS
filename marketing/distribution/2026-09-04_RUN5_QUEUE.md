## First-party measurement now available
ProofBerry Base44 records anonymous `GrowthEvent` rows for:
- `landing_view`
- `waitlist_form_start`
- `waitlist_submit`

Each event carries anonymous session ID plus source, campaign and content variant. This allows landing-to-form-start and landing-to-waitlist conversion analysis without waiting for a Google Analytics connection.
