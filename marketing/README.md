# PROOFBERRY AI — Autonomous Marketing Vault

Primary conversion goal: **waitlist signups**. Paid customers are a secondary bonus.

## Three launch audiences
1. **Job seekers / career changers** — ProofBerry turns a flat resume into verifiable, agent-readable evidence of capability and a stage where candidates can demonstrate what they can do.
2. **Recruiters / staffing firms / talent acquisition teams** — ProofBerry helps surface verified capability rather than forcing decisions from resume keywords alone.
3. **Employers / workforce-development & training organizations** — ProofBerry provides role-specific skills verification, simulations, training evidence, reporting, and an accountable AI workforce layer.

## Core candidate thesis
A job seeker is marketing a product: themselves and their capabilities. The resume is a legacy marketing asset. In an agentic hiring environment, ProofBerry is designed to package, verify, demonstrate, and surface capability to the humans and automated systems deciding who advances to an interview.

## Continuous Growth OS
Customer Truth → Founder/Brand Content → Outbound/Distribution Signals → Creative Testing → AI Search Visibility → Growth Cockpit → Evaluation Loop → Winner Library → next experiments.

Every asset must record: product, audience, funnel stage, channel, format, hook, CTA, version, date, status, source, performance, and reusable recipe tags.

## Operating rule
Never optimize merely for volume. Optimize for qualified attention and waitlist conversion. Maintain truthful claims. No fabricated testimonials, customers, outcomes, hiring guarantees, or performance statistics.
