ProofBerry is designed to add structured evidence: demonstrated skills, role-specific verification, simulations, interview readiness, work samples and capability signals that can help tell a richer story to the humans and automated systems evaluating talent.

**From claims → evidence.** Show structured proof of capability instead of relying exclusively on self-description.
