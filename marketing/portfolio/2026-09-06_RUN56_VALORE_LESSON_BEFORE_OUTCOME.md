# Valoré Mastery Institute — Run 56 — Prove the Lesson Before the Outcome

## Customer Truth
An education product should earn trust through curriculum, learning workflow, and clearly defined program terms before it leans on adoption counts, testimonials, credential recognition, career outcomes, representation, or collector-access promises.

## Creative Strategy
**Hook:** A learning product should prove the lesson before it promises the outcome.

**Body:** Valoré has an observable eight-module curriculum and a defined study path across 30 historical and modern artists. Run 56 moved the public experience toward those inspectable surfaces. Unsupported adoption/satisfaction counts were replaced with product facts; public testimonial quotes were withheld pending source and consent verification; certificate language now identifies a program-issued preview rather than an accredited or independently recognized credential; representation is framed as a planned selective pathway rather than a guaranteed outcome; and displayed pricing is labeled proposed packaging because checkout, refund handling, reviewer staffing, and enrollment terms were not independently verified.

**CTA:** Review the eight-module curriculum, certificate terms, review model, and program packaging before deciding whether enrollment fits.
