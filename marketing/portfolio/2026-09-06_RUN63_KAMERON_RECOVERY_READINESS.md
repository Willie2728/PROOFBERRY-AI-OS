## Before You Price Recovery, Define What Recovery Must Prove

### Customer Truth
A serious AI-agent recovery buyer needs an observable test definition before comparing package prices. Recovery language is not evidence that checkpointing, side-effect protection, resume authority, recovery objectives, or enforcement adapters are operating.

### Creative Strategy
**Hook:** Before you price recovery, define what recovery must prove.

AI-agent recovery is not a pricing problem first. It is a proof problem. What state gets checkpointed? Which side effects must not repeat? Who can authorize resume? What interruption window matters? Which runtime and enforcement adapters are actually connected? Define those five things before comparing packages. Then decide whether a bounded recovery pilot is worth scoping.

**CTA:** Use the five-question buyer-readiness checklist to scope a bounded KAMERON recovery evaluation.
