## Creative Strategy
**VTR-TXT-001 — Before you buy identity verification, define what the verification path must prove.**

CTA: Inspect the workflow, define the assurance/risk requirement, list the providers and evidence required, then decide whether a production integration review is warranted.

## Production Readiness
**VTR-LP-001** added a qualified-integration checklist to the VerifyTrust landing CTA. Before treating the flow as production-ready, the buyer is prompted to define:
1. the identity-proofing risk or assurance level the use case requires;
2. the document, liveness, biometric, fraud and video providers actually connected and tested;
3. accuracy/error-rate evidence applicable to conditions similar to the intended operating environment;
4. privacy, retention, consent, access and deletion rules for identity evidence;
5. the escalation path for inconclusive or mismatched evidence;
6. the audit receipt that proves what provider and decision path actually ran and what remains unverified.

The UI explicitly labels the checklist as buyer-enablement guidance rather than a certification, compliance, accuracy or production-provider claim.
