# RUN91 — AvatarConnect: Consent + Provider Before Likeness Scale

## Customer Truth
A digital-twin concept is not a live avatar service. Before asking for contact information, a serious buyer should define one bounded avatar use case, the authority basis for the likeness, provider/runtime readiness, a decision window, and the evidence required before expanding.

## Creative Strategy
**Hook:** Prove one avatar workflow before you scale the likeness.

The conversion path now asks the buyer to define:
- one bounded avatar use case;
- whether the likeness is their own, an authorized team member's, licensed talent, or not yet ready;
- provider/runtime status;
- whether a consent workflow is ready;
- a decision window; and
- the first-workflow receipt required before expansion.

**CTA:** Record one-avatar workflow request only after the scope and authority boundary are defined.
