# That One AI Podcast — Run 62

## Campaign
**An RSS feed is not a live audience.**

Publish the verified episode. Create or enable the RSS feed. Submit or connect it where each destination requires. Then open the public listing before you call that channel live. Distribution is a chain of verified handoffs, not a checkbox that says “everywhere.”

CTA: **Verify each public listing before you promote the show.**

## Customer Truth
The connected launch page contained stale distribution instructions: Spotify for Podcasters branding, a Google Podcasts URL, a statement that YouTube Music imports automatically, a blanket 1–5 day approval claim, and language implying that approval makes the show live across every major platform.

## Product Correction
The connected Base44 Launch surface now uses Spotify for Creators, Apple Podcasts Connect, and YouTube Studio Podcasts. It explains that Spotify-hosted shows publish automatically to Spotify but require separate submission to other platforms; Apple performs technical validation and review; YouTube RSS ingestion is handled in YouTube Studio for eligible channels/regions; and each public listing should be opened and verified before being marked live.
