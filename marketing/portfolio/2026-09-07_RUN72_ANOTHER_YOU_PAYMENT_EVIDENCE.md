# RUN72 — Another You — Payment Evidence Before Earnings

## Customer Truth
A pipeline label is not a payment receipt, and seeded demo opportunities are not production traction.

## Creative Strategy
**Hook:** Don’t call budget earned until the payment evidence says so.

**Deployable copy:**
Another You is designed to carry an opportunity from source review through delivery and payment tracking without collapsing planning state into proof. Moving a job card to a pipeline stage no longer manufactures payment evidence. A human can record that payment was received, while provider-verified payment remains a separate state reserved for a connected provider receipt. Seeded `.example` opportunities are now explicitly marked as demo records and excluded from operating metrics.

**CTA:** Track one real opportunity from source verification to delivered work to payment evidence.
