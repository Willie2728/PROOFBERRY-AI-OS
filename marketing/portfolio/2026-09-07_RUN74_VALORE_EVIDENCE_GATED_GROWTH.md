# RUN74 — Valoré Mastery Institute — Evidence-Gated Growth

## Customer Truth
A planning model is not a growth result, and an unverified testimonial is not social proof. Serious artists should be able to inspect the learning surface and operating terms before Valoré markets enrollment, revenue, representation, or student outcomes.

## Creative Strategy
**VMI-TXT-001 — Inspect the learning before you buy the promise.**

Hook: **The strongest art-school ad is not a revenue projection. It is a learning surface a serious artist can inspect.**

Body:
Valoré already has an inspectable eight-module program preview, certificate terms, and human-review boundaries. RUN74 moves the growth dashboard toward that same standard: modeled economics are labeled scenario math, legacy testimonials stay withheld until source/consent/outcome evidence is verified, and scale decisions are gated behind attribution, payment, delivery, reviewer-capacity, and learner-outcome receipts.

CTA: **Preview the eight-module path. Review the certificate and human-review boundaries. Enroll only after the live offer is verified.**
