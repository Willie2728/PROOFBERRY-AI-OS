# ProofBerry Run 6 — Customer Truth: Trust Is the Bottleneck

Date: 2026-09-04

## Signal
The hiring market increasingly has two simultaneous problems: more AI-assisted applications and lower confidence in whether application signals reflect real capability.

Current evidence used for this run:
- Robert Half (2026-03-10): 67% of surveyed US HR leaders said AI-generated applications are slowing hiring; 65% of hiring managers said AI-enhanced resumes make candidate skills harder to verify.
- Greenhouse 2026 AI in Hiring reporting: 62% of recruiters report increased application volume, while only 21% are very confident their systems are not filtering out qualified candidates.
- Gartner (2026-07-03): growing candidate use of AI to tailor resumes is contributing to skills inflation and higher recruiter workload; Gartner recommends redesigning recruiting for transparency rather than simply banning candidate AI.

## Customer truth
### Candidate
"I can use AI to improve how I present myself, but I still need a credible way to show I actually know the work."

### Recruiter / staffing
"I do not need more polished applications. I need stronger signals that help me decide who deserves human attention."

### Employer / workforce organization
"I need evidence mapped to role requirements, without handing final judgment to a black box."

## Messaging implication
ProofBerry should not attack AI-assisted applications. It should own the next layer:

**AI can improve presentation. ProofBerry is being built to improve signal quality.**

ProofBerry's category claim for testing:
**Capability evidence for AI-assisted hiring.**

## Guardrails
Do not claim ProofBerry guarantees interviews, jobs, recruiter visibility, reduced time-to-hire, fraud detection, or superior hiring outcomes without measured product evidence. Keep human decision authority explicit.
