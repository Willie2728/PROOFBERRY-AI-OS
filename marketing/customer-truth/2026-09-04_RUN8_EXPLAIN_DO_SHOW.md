# ProofBerry Growth Run 8 — Customer Truth: Explain → Do → Show

## Fresh signal
Robert Half reported March 10, 2026 that 67% of surveyed U.S. HR leaders said AI-generated applications were slowing hiring and 65% of hiring managers said AI-enhanced applications made candidate skills harder to verify.

SHRM Labs reported in 2026 that an analysis of nearly 20,000 interviews from July 2025–January 2026 found 38.5% showed signs of AI-assisted cheating. The SHRM article emphasizes a practical signal: a genuine high performer can explain the thinking behind an answer, while scenario-based assessment and follow-up questions can expose weak or borrowed reasoning.

- https://www.shrm.org/labs/resources/when-candidates-can-fake-skills-real-time-assessment-design

## Marketing conclusion
Do not frame AI use itself as the enemy. The sharper problem is low-confidence signal. ProofBerry should market three inspectable layers:
1. **Explain it** — can the candidate articulate the reasoning?
2. **Do it** — can the candidate perform a role-relevant task or scenario?
3. **Show it** — can the evidence be surfaced to a recruiter or employer in a structured way?

## Funnel observation
First-party GrowthEvent telemetry currently shows 7 landing-view events across 5 unique browser sessions and no recorded form-start or waitlist-submit events in the current telemetry window. This sample is far too small to declare a conversion problem, and some traffic may be internal, but it justifies a low-risk friction test.

## Run 8 conversion change
- Moved the Early Access Waitlist directly below the hero instead of near the bottom of the long homepage.
- Made name optional; email remains required and marketing consent remains explicit.
- Added a short friction-reduction message: roughly 20 seconds, no payment required.
- Preserved first-party attribution and event telemetry.

## Claims rule
No claim that ProofBerry prevents fraud, guarantees interviews, guarantees hiring, or verifies identity unless that specific capability is demonstrably implemented and substantiated.
