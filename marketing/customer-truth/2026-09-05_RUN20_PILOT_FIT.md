# Run 20 Customer Truth — Pilot Fit

## Decision
Move the recruiter/staffing message from another feature explanation toward **pilot qualification**: `one live role → small cohort → compare current client handoff with an evidence packet → decide whether the workflow earned a second role`.

## Fresh evidence reviewed
- Robert Half (March 10, 2026): 65% of surveyed hiring managers said AI-enhanced resumes made skills harder to verify. 67% of HR leaders reported using staffing firms; 89% of those users said staffing partners were effective in addressing AI-related hiring challenges.
- Bullhorn (May 28, 2026): Amplify Digital Workers expand automation across staffing workflows, including verification and multi-step execution.
- Greenhouse Real Talent (current 2026 product page): identity verification, fraud detection and matching are being integrated into hiring platforms while employers retain control of hiring decisions.
- SHRM Labs (current 2026 guidance): role-specific scenarios and follow-up reasoning are useful ways to examine applied capability instead of relying only on polished responses.

## Customer truth synthesis
The buyer does not need ProofBerry to replace ATS/CRM, identity verification or staffing automation. A sharper wedge is to test whether one live role becomes easier for a client to review when the handoff contains explicit criteria, candidate work evidence, follow-up reasoning, reviewer notes and open questions.

## Messaging guardrails
- Do not imply ProofBerry verifies identity unless that exact function is operational and evidenced.
- Do not claim elimination of bias, fraud, cheating or bad hires.
- Do not claim predictive validity, placement lift, time-to-fill improvement or revenue impact without measured customer data.
- Keep consequential hiring decisions human-owned.
