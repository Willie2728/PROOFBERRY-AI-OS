# ProofBerry Customer Truth — Growth Run 7

## Signal
The strongest recurring hiring-market problem is no longer simply application volume. It is **signal trust** in a market where AI can improve both candidate presentation and employer screening.

Fresh/primary evidence reviewed:
- Robert Half, March 10, 2026: 67% of surveyed U.S. HR leaders said AI-generated applications were slowing hiring; 65% of hiring managers said AI-enhanced resumes made skills harder to verify. Source: https://press.roberthalf.com/2026-03-10-Robert-Half-survey-67-of-HR-leaders-report-AI-generated-applications-are-slowing-hiring
- Indeed, 2026: recommends explainable assistive AI, objective evaluation instructions, candidate feedback/appeal paths, and humans in the loop for decisions. Source: https://uk.indeed.com/lead/what-candidates-want-employers-to-get-right-about-ai
- Greenhouse 2026 AI in Hiring report public summary: recruiters report increased application volume while confidence in automated filtering remains limited; trust and transparency are central issues.

## Run 7 positioning
**AI is not the enemy. Weak, opaque signal is the problem.**

ProofBerry should be positioned as a trust layer for the agentic hiring era: organize role-relevant capability evidence so candidates can be understood by people and AI-assisted systems while keeping decisions human.

## Messaging rules
- Do not imply AI screening is always automatic rejection.
- Do not attack legitimate candidate AI assistance.
- Do not promise interviews, jobs, recruiter views, or hiring outcomes.
- Do emphasize inspectable evidence, role relevance, transparent evaluation, and human authority.

## Testable statement
"The hiring arms race changed. AI helps people apply faster. AI helps teams screen faster. Speed is not the missing layer. Trust is."
