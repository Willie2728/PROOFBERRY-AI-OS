# ProofBerry AI — Run 31 Customer Truth: Contradiction Queue

Date: 2026-09-05

## Customer truth

**Do not make every candidate complete another assessment. Investigate the places where relevant signals conflict.**

The Run 30 evidence-burden budget established a useful rule: deeper proof should be reserved for unresolved, role-critical questions. Run 31 makes that routing logic concrete.

SHRM's 2026 guidance on real-time AI-assisted skillfishing describes a useful assessment-design pattern: discrepancies between strong assessment performance and weak follow-up explanations can be a signal that warrants closer review. The article also notes that a genuine high performer should be able to explain their thinking. This is hiring-market evidence, not a ProofBerry fraud-detection result.

StaffingHub's 2026 reporting continues to show that staffing firms face rising client pressure around candidate verification as AI-generated applications make skills harder to verify. The practical implication is not to label disagreement as fraud; it is to make disagreement inspectable and route it to a narrower human follow-up.

## Pilot method

`candidate-stated signal → demonstrated / observed signal → aligned, conflicting, or unclear → one targeted follow-up only when needed → unresolved review note → human-owned decision`

Design principles:
- A contradiction is a **review cue**, not a fraud verdict.
- Aligned evidence should stop additional candidate burden unless another role-critical question remains.
- A follow-up should be tied to the specific disagreement, not become a generic second interview.
- Preserve evidence source and context so the staffing client can see why a question was escalated.
- Keep the consequential hiring decision with the recruiter or client reviewer.

Connected ProofBerry still has **0 RubricCriterion records**, so this remains an illustrative early-access design-partner method rather than a finalized rubric, validated discrepancy detector, predictive selection model, compliance certification, or automatic hiring system.
