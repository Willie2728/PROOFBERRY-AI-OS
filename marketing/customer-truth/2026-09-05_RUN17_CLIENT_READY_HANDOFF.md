# ProofBerry Customer Truth — Run 17: Client-Ready Evidence Handoff

Date: 2026-09-05
Audience: staffing firms, recruiting agencies, workforce teams

## Customer Truth

The staffing buyer does not only need a faster way to evaluate a candidate. A staffing recruiter also needs a credible way to hand that evaluation to a client without turning the recruiter into another black-box score distributor.

Run 17 thesis:

> **Don’t just send a candidate. Send the evidence a client can review.**

The strongest ProofBerry staffing wedge is a client-ready handoff that preserves recruiter judgment and human decision authority while making the underlying candidate signal easier to inspect.

## Implication for ProofBerry

For staffing and recruiting teams, the highest-value early-access workflow is not a generic “AI score.” It is a structured handoff:

`role criterion → candidate evidence → follow-up reasoning → recruiter note → client/human decision`

That handoff gives the recruiter something concrete to discuss with the client while preserving the relationship-heavy work that staffing firms are already spending more time doing.

## Conversion Hypothesis

A staffing-specific design-partner offer will attract higher-quality recruiter leads than generic early-access language when it asks for one live role and one candidate-review problem rather than asking a buyer to imagine the entire future platform.
