# Growth Run 14 — Customer Truth: The Review Packet

## Market signal

ProofBerry should move one step closer to the recruiter's actual decision workflow: not another generic score, but a compact review packet that makes role criteria, observed evidence, uncertainty, and follow-up questions inspectable before the live interview.

### Current 2026 evidence

- Greenhouse, August 7, 2026, reports that recruiting teams shrank 56% between 2022 and 2025 while hiring work remained. Its guidance for AI agents in HR is to automate repetitive work while preserving structured hiring foundations, governance controls, candidate experience, and human-owned final decisions.
- SHRM, July 7, 2026, warns that keeping a human as the final decision-maker does not remove the need to validate AI-enabled selection processes. Human ownership and measurement quality are separate requirements.
- SHRM Labs' 2026 assessment-design coverage emphasizes scenario-based work and follow-up reasoning because candidates who genuinely understand the work should be able to explain their thinking rather than only present polished answers.

## Customer tension

Recruiters need to save time without surrendering judgment. Candidates need to understand what is being evaluated without being reduced to a black-box score. Employers need documentation that helps a human reviewer ask better questions rather than pretending software can make the hiring decision alone.

## Run 14 thesis

**The recruiter does not need another score. They need a review packet.**

Useful pre-interview handoff:

1. Role criterion.
2. Observed evidence.
3. What remains uncertain.
4. The next question a human should ask.
5. Explicit human decision ownership.

This turns ProofBerry from a generic verification message into a concrete decision-support artifact.

## Claims boundary

Do not claim that ProofBerry independently validates legal compliance, eliminates bias, predicts job performance, guarantees a better hire, or replaces human judgment. The Run 14 creative is an illustrative workflow concept grounded in current product direction.
