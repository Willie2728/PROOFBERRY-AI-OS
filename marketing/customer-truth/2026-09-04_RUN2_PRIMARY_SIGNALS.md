# ProofBerry Customer Truth — Primary-Source Update (Run 2)

Date: 2026-09-04

## Primary market signals

### 1. Recruiters are facing application overload, not a shortage of applicants
Indeed reported on June 15, 2026 that 71% of surveyed U.S. hiring managers at companies with 500+ employees said higher application volume made it harder to find qualified candidates, and 72% feared missing top talent because of the volume they had to review.

Source: https://www.indeed.com/news/releases/indeeds-ai-powered-sourcing-assistant-helps-employers-hire-over-30-faster

**ProofBerry implication:** recruiter messaging should not promise "more candidates." The sharper B2B wedge is **better signal, faster triage, and clearer evidence for the candidates already in the funnel.**

### 2. Screening is moving toward recruiter-defined criteria and explainable alignment
Indeed's Smart Screening describes an AI-assisted flow in which recruiters define job criteria, applicants are reviewed against those criteria, and the system shows where a candidate aligns and where gaps may exist while keeping recruiters responsible for hiring decisions.

Source: https://ca.indeed.com/hire/resources/howtohub/what-is-indeed-smart-screening

**ProofBerry implication:** emphasize **structured evidence mapped to role requirements** and **human decision authority**, not "AI replaces recruiters."

### 3. Skills translation is becoming a product category
Workday announced its Military Skills Mapper on February 3, 2026 to translate military experience into civilian-equivalent skills inside Workday Recruiting.

Source: https://newsroom.workday.com/2026-02-03-Workday-Introduces-the-Military-Skills-Mapper-to-Help-Organizations-Better-Recognize-and-Hire-Veteran-Talent

**ProofBerry implication:** career changers, veterans, returning workers, and people with unconventional experience are a strong candidate wedge. The message is: **your experience may be valuable even when the resume language does not translate cleanly into the role language.**

### 4. Responsible AI positioning matters
Workday publishes bias-testing and responsible-AI documentation for candidate matching, and Indeed explicitly frames automated screening as assistive while recruiters retain decision authority.

Sources:
- https://www.workday.com/en-us/legal/responsible-ai-and-bias-mitigation.html
- https://ca.indeed.com/hire/resources/howtohub/what-is-indeed-smart-screening

**ProofBerry implication:** marketing should repeatedly show the system as an **evidence and decision-support layer**, with transparent criteria and human control.

## Customer language to test

### Candidate / career changer
- "Your experience may be stronger than the words used to describe it."
- "Translate what you've done into evidence of what you can do next."
- "A resume is an introduction. Your capability needs a stage."
- "Don't optimize only for keywords. Build proof around the role."

### Recruiter / staffing
- "You don't need another pile of applicants. You need clearer signal."
- "Map role criteria to evidence, then let humans make the decision."
- "Application volume is high. Qualified signal is scarce."

### Employer / workforce organization
- "Translate experience into role-ready capability evidence."
- "Define the criteria. See the evidence. Keep the decision human."
- "Use simulations and structured proof to understand capability beyond job titles."

## Claims guardrails

Do not claim ProofBerry guarantees interviews, predicts job performance with certainty, eliminates bias, replaces recruiters, defeats ATS systems, or is already integrated with systems that are not verified. Use "designed to," "helps," "structured evidence," "role-relevant," and "decision support" unless ProofBerry has measured data supporting stronger claims.
