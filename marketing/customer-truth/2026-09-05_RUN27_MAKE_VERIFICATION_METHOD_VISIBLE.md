# ProofBerry Customer Truth — Run 27

## Buyer truth

**Don’t just tell the client you verify candidates. Make the verification method visible.**

Staffing firms are increasingly being asked to help employers deal with application volume and harder-to-verify skills. Robert Half reported on March 10, 2026 that 65% of surveyed hiring managers said AI-enhanced resumes made skills harder to verify; 67% of HR leaders said they were using staffing firms, and 89% of those users said staffing partners were effective at addressing AI-related hiring challenges. StaffingHub’s April 28, 2026 staffing-industry commentary argues that firms should make candidate-validation methodology visible to clients rather than treating verification as an invisible background step.

This suggests a lower-risk ProofBerry design-partner wedge:

`role signal → comparable evidence → uncertainty/open question → recruiter context → client review → human-owned decision`

The marketing job is not to claim a validated ProofBerry hiring model. It is to make the proposed pilot method inspectable so a staffing buyer can judge whether the handoff is worth testing on one live role.
