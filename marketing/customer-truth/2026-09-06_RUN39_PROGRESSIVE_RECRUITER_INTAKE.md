# ProofBerry Growth Run 39 — Customer Truth

## Customer Truth

**Do not make an explorer fill out a pilot application.**

The current first-party funnel is still extremely small and should not be treated as a statistically meaningful conversion result: 22 GrowthEvent records total, with 19 landing views, 3 waitlist-form views, 0 audience selections, 0 form starts, and 0 submits at the start of Run 39. Some activity may be internal or preview traffic. The useful signal is therefore a design hypothesis, not a conclusion: the next recruiter-facing form should reduce unnecessary cognitive load before the first meaningful interaction while preserving stronger qualification for buyers who actually have a live role.

## Buyer segmentation rule

Recruiter / staffing traffic now has two honest states:

1. **I have a live role to test** — reveal the optional one-role design-partner readiness fields: active requisition, named pilot owner, and finite 14/30/60-day decision window.
2. **I’m exploring options** — keep the early-access intake lightweight and classify the buyer as general interest rather than design-partner-ready.

A staffing visitor should not have to pretend to be pilot-ready in order to join early access, and general interest should not be counted as a qualified design-partner signal.

## Market context

Robert Half reported on March 10, 2026 that 67% of surveyed U.S. HR leaders said reviewing AI-generated applications had slowed hiring and 65% of hiring managers said AI-enhanced resumes made skills harder to verify. The same survey reported that 67% of respondents were using staffing firms and 89% of those respondents said those partners had been effective in addressing AI-related hiring challenges. These are category findings, not ProofBerry outcomes.

## Conversion implication

The near-term ProofBerry job is not to maximize raw form completion at any cost. It is to create a low-friction path for exploratory staffing interest while making a **3/3 design-partner-ready** submission meaningfully different: one live role, one accountable owner, and one finite evaluation window.

## Claims boundary

This operating method does not establish validated selection performance, fraud or identity verification, compliance, bias elimination, predictive validity, ATS integration, placement improvement, ROI, or automatic hiring. Consequential hiring decisions remain human-owned.
