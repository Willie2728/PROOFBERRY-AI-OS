# ProofBerry AI — Growth Run 32 Customer Truth

Date: 2026-09-05
Status: durable research record

## Customer Truth

ProofBerry has accumulated enough conceptual awareness creative. The conversion problem is now implementation clarity: a staffing buyer needs to know exactly what a low-risk first test requires, what it returns, what remains human-owned, and how the firm decides whether to continue.

Bullhorn's 2026 Industry Trends work reports that only 10% of firms have AI embedded throughout the workflow, while data/security remain major obstacles and 20% of firms say they lack a clear implementation plan. Bullhorn also advises firms to start with small targeted bets rather than wait for perfect data. Greenhouse's 2026 Candidate AI Interview Report found that candidate trust and transparency can materially affect continuation when AI is introduced into hiring.

## Growth implication

Lead with a bounded design-partner contract, not an AI-transformation promise:

`one active requisition → one staffing owner → one client reviewer → existing workflow retained → proposed evidence-packet prototype → measured review usefulness / effort / candidate friction → expand, revise, or stop`

The offer should make non-goals explicit: no automatic hiring, no claimed ATS replacement, no guaranteed placement, no ROI promise, no compliance certification, and no implication that the current early-access method is a validated selection model.

## Run 32 decision

Create one deployable buyer-facing offer card plus one outreach text asset. Keep the landing architecture stable until attributable recruiter/staffing traffic exists. Do not create another broad awareness family or promote a Winner Library asset without minimum-sample evidence.
