# ProofBerry Growth Run 15 — Customer Truth: Show the Criteria First

Date: 2026-09-05
Owner: WC05 Customer Truth

## Customer truth

AI-assisted hiring is creating a second trust question beyond whether a candidate is real or capable: **does the candidate understand what is being evaluated and how the evidence will be used?**

Greenhouse's 2026 candidate research reports that 70% of U.S. candidates who had experienced AI evaluation said the AI evaluation was not clearly disclosed before their most recent AI interview. Greenhouse's August 4, 2026 AI-interviewing guidance also frames AI as support for repetitive work while recruiters and hiring managers remain responsible for the conversations and decisions that move hiring forward.

SHRM's July 7, 2026 validation guidance reinforces the buyer-side obligation to ask whether candidates are evaluated under comparable conditions, whether they have a fair opportunity to demonstrate capability, whether the process measures what it is supposed to measure, and whether validation is maintained over time.

Sources:
- Greenhouse Candidate AI Interview Report / analysis: https://www.greenhouse.com/blog/2026-candidate-ai-interview-report
- Greenhouse, "What is AI interviewing?", 2026-08-04: https://www.greenhouse.com/resources/glossary/ai-interviewing
- SHRM, "Is Your AI Hiring Tool Discriminating? Here's How to Find Out", 2026-07-07: https://www.shrm.org/in/topics-tools/news/ai-hiring-tools-discrimination-how-to-validate

## Implication for ProofBerry

The useful acquisition wedge is not "AI is bad" and not "our score is better." It is **evaluation transparency**:

1. Define the role criterion.
2. Tell the candidate what evidence is being requested.
3. Make AI's assistance legible.
4. Keep the consequential decision human-owned.

Working message: **Show the criteria before the score.**
