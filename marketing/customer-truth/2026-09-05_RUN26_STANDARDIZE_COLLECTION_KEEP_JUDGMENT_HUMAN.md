# ProofBerry Customer Truth — Run 26

Date: 2026-09-05
Thesis: **Standardize the evidence collection. Keep the consequential judgment human.**

## Why this matters now

A July 30, 2026 natural field experiment involving about 70,000 applicants compared AI voice interviews with human recruiter interviews while keeping final evaluation and hiring decisions with human recruiters. The researchers report that the AI-interview condition collected hiring-relevant information more consistently and was associated with improved downstream outcomes in that setting. This is useful product-design evidence for standardizing information collection, not a guarantee that ProofBerry will reproduce those results.

ManpowerGroup's March 3, 2026 rollout of AI interviewing uses a similar human-first operating principle: AI can handle parts of early screening while recruiters remain at the center of hiring decisions.

CV-Library's April 15, 2026 recruiter/candidate survey also found significant candidate concern about opaque AI involvement, reinforcing the need to explain where AI is used and where human review remains accountable.

Sources:

- CV-Library, `The 2026 AI in Recruitment survey`, 2026-04-15: https://www.cv-library.co.uk/recruitment-insight/ai-in-recruitment-survey/

## Buyer implication

ProofBerry should not sell a staffing design partner on an AI verdict. The lower-risk wedge is a one-role pilot that standardizes what evidence is collected and how uncertainty is recorded, while leaving advancement, rejection, hiring and other consequential decisions with accountable humans.

Pilot operating model:

`role criterion → common evidence prompt → candidate artifact / explanation → uncertainty / follow-up → recruiter review → human decision`

## Product truth boundary

The connected ProofBerry app currently has no configured `RubricCriterion` records and no verified evidence that standardized collection improves hiring outcomes in ProofBerry specifically. Run 26 therefore markets an illustrative pilot protocol, not a validated selection system, compliance claim, bias-elimination claim, predictive-validity claim, automatic hiring claim, or guaranteed ROI/time-to-hire improvement.
