# ProofBerry Customer Truth — 2026-09-04

## What changed / what matters

The market is increasingly teaching both sides of hiring to think in terms of structured evidence rather than resume keywords alone.

- Greenhouse added AI-assisted Talent Matching in 2026; candidate applications can be organized against recruiter-defined criteria before human review. Source: https://www.jobscan.co/blog/can-ats-detect-ai-resume/
- Recruiting vendors are explicitly positioning around evidence-backed screening, requirement-by-requirement scoring, explainable candidate summaries, and audit trails rather than raw keyword matching. Source: https://tersen.ai/
- Candidate-side products now openly tell job seekers to "beat" employer AI by optimizing resumes for screening systems. Source: https://massapply.ai/
- A 2026 hiring experiment with 1,700 recruiters in the US and UK found AI skills increased interview invitation probabilities by roughly 8–15 percentage points across the tested occupations, reinforcing that specific, legible capability signals can materially affect screening. Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.13286

## Customer language to use

### Candidate
- "Before you can win the job, you have to win the right to be seen."
- "Your resume is one marketing asset. It should not be your entire proof."
- "Don't only describe capability. Package evidence of it."
- "The hiring stack is becoming agentic. Your professional story must be legible to people and systems."

### Recruiter / staffing
- "A hundred resumes can claim experience. Which candidates can show evidence?"
- "Keep humans in control while giving them better structured signal."
- "Move from keyword triage toward explainable capability evidence."

### Employer / workforce organization
- "Map role requirements to demonstrated capability, not just self-description."
- "Train, simulate, verify, and document role-relevant evidence."
- "Create a defensible evidence layer around workforce decisions."

## Claims guardrails

Do not say ProofBerry guarantees interviews, defeats ATS systems, eliminates bias, or proves someone can perform a job with certainty. Use "designed to," "helps," "structured evidence," "verification," and "demonstrated capability" unless stronger claims are supported by measured ProofBerry data.

## Current campaign hypothesis

The strongest candidate wedge is not "better resume." It is **resume + verifiable capability evidence for an agentic hiring environment**. The strongest B2B wedge is **better signal and auditability without removing human decision authority**.
