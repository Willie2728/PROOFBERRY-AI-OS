# ProofBerry Growth Run 16 — Customer Truth

## Thesis
**Staffing firms are a high-value wedge because AI is increasing application complexity while recruiters are spending more time on human conversation and judgment.**

## Fresh evidence
- American Staffing Association, June 4, 2026: recruiter call time reached 286 minutes per week in Q1 2026, the highest level reported in the cited Staffing Productivity Report, while average AI-tool use also increased.
- Robert Half, March 10, 2026: 67% of surveyed U.S. HR leaders said they were using staffing firms for hiring support; 89% said those partners were effective in addressing AI-related hiring challenges. Robert Half also reported 65% of hiring managers said AI-enhanced resumes made skills harder to verify.
- SHRM Labs 2026 assessment guidance argues that applied reasoning and candidates' ability to explain how they reached an answer are more useful signals than pure recall when AI can manufacture polished responses.

## Customer language to use
- "Give the client evidence they can inspect."
- "Let recruiters spend human time where human judgment matters."
- "Role criterion → candidate artifact → follow-up → reviewer note."

## Boundary
Do not claim ProofBerry shortens time-to-fill, improves quality-of-hire, eliminates bias, prevents cheating, or guarantees placement. The live RubricCriterion entity still has no finalized criterion records, so criteria-first examples remain illustrative early-access concepts until criteria are configured and validated.
