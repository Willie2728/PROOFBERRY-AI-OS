# ProofBerry AI — Growth Run 35 Customer Truth

Date: 2026-09-05

## Customer Truth

**Disclosure is step one. Give the candidate a path back.**

The next conversion question after candidate transparency is whether the candidate can reach an accountable human when the AI-assisted step is confusing, evidence is misunderstood, or an unusual support/access need appears.

Greenhouse's May 1, 2026 Candidate AI Interview Report says 70% of surveyed US candidates who experienced AI evaluation were not clearly told before their most recent AI interview, 38% had withdrawn from a process that included an AI interview, 39% wanted a clear explanation of what AI was measuring, 38% wanted confirmation that a person reviews AI output before decisions, and 46% wanted the option to request a human interview instead. These are category signals, not ProofBerry outcomes.

Source: https://www.greenhouse.com/blog/2026-candidate-ai-interview-report

## Design implication

For one staffing design-partner role, define before collection:
1. where AI is used;
2. who can answer candidate questions;
3. how candidate clarification is preserved if evidence is misunderstood;
4. who handles unusual access/support/process exceptions;
5. who owns the consequential decision and unresolved questions.

Do not present these as universal legal rights. Available choices and obligations depend on the employer process and applicable requirements.

## Conversion objective

Qualify staffing buyers that can make the candidate-facing process inspectable before launch. Optimize for one attributable, reviewable design-partner role rather than broad traffic volume.
