# ProofBerry AI — Growth Run 18 Customer Truth

Date: 2026-09-05
Audience: staffing agency owners, recruiting leaders, client-facing recruiters

## Thesis

**Pilot one live role. Not your whole recruiting stack.**

ProofBerry's current buyer wedge should reduce adoption risk: let a staffing firm test whether structured, role-relevant evidence improves the recruiter-to-client handoff on one real role before asking the firm to change a broader workflow.

## Fresh market evidence

- American Staffing Association, June 4, 2026: recruiter call time reached 286 minutes per week in Q1 2026 while average AI-tool use also increased. The association framed relationship-building as a continuing cornerstone as firms adopt more AI.
- Bullhorn, May 28, 2026: staffing automation is increasingly handling repetitive work including screening and preparing submittals; Bullhorn also introduced verification-oriented digital-worker capabilities. This increases the value of a clear, human-reviewable candidate handoff rather than another generic speed promise.
- Greenhouse, June 10, 2026: applications were reported up 129% since 2023 while open roles remained roughly flat; Greenhouse emphasized clearer, source-linked hiring signals and structured workflows rather than speed without clarity.
- Robert Half, March 10, 2026: 65% of surveyed hiring managers said AI-enhanced resumes made candidate skills harder to verify; 67% of surveyed HR leaders reported using staffing firms and 89% of those users said staffing partners were effective at addressing AI-related hiring challenges.

## Buyer objection translated into product strategy

A staffing leader does not need to believe a new platform can transform the whole agency. A better design-partner question is narrower:

1. Can we choose one live role?
2. Can recruiter and client agree on a small set of role-relevant evidence questions?
3. Can a small candidate cohort produce explanations, demonstrations, or artifacts that help review?
4. Can the recruiter hand the client an inspectable packet containing evidence, recruiter notes, and unresolved follow-up questions?
5. Does the team want to repeat the workflow on a second role?

## Measurement guardrails

Measure buyer usefulness, candidate completion/drop-off, reviewer clarity, unresolved-question quality, and willingness to repeat. Do not claim automatic hiring, bias elimination, guaranteed placement speed, predictive job-performance validity, revenue lift, or legal compliance without independent evidence.
