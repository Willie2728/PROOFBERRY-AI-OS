# ProofBerry Customer Truth — Run 4: Skill-Signal Integrity

Date: 2026-09-04

## Signal
Hiring teams are dealing with two simultaneous pressures: rising application volume and growing concern that AI-assisted applications and assessments can overstate candidate capability. That creates a strong category opening for ProofBerry: **make capability evidence easier to inspect, explain, and trust without pretending automation should make the hiring decision.**

## Current evidence
- Indeed reported June 15, 2026 that 71% of surveyed hiring managers at companies with 500+ employees said higher application volume made qualified candidates harder to find; 72% feared missing top talent in the volume.
- Gartner's July 3, 2026 research abstract says growing candidate AI use to tailor resumes is fueling skills inflation and application volume, increasing recruiter workload.
- SHRM's 2026 coverage of assessment design describes growing concern about candidates using AI during assessments and emphasizes follow-up questions and scenario-based evidence that reveal whether a candidate can explain their thinking.
- SHRM's July 7, 2026 coverage also stresses that AI-enabled hiring processes still need validation and human accountability; a human final decision does not erase risk introduced earlier in the funnel.

## Customer truth by audience
### Candidate
"I use AI to help me compete, but I still need a way to show that the capability is actually mine."

### Recruiter
"I don't need more polished claims. I need evidence I can interrogate and understand."

### Employer
"I want faster screening, but I cannot outsource accountability or quality to a black box."

## ProofBerry positioning implication
Shift one major campaign family from **resume-vs-keywords** to **signal integrity**:

> AI can help you present yourself. ProofBerry is being built to help you prove yourself.

For recruiters:

> Don't ask whether the application looks polished. Ask whether the candidate can explain, demonstrate, and support the claim.

## Claims guardrail
Do not claim ProofBerry detects cheating, prevents fraud, eliminates bad hires, or verifies identity unless the exact product workflow and validation evidence support those claims. The permitted claim is narrower: ProofBerry is being designed around structured capability evidence, role-relevant demonstrations, follow-up evaluation, and human decision authority.
