# ProofBerry AI — Customer Truth — Run 37

## Customer truth

**Qualified interest is not the same as design-partner readiness.**

A staffing buyer can be genuinely interested in ProofBerry without yet having a measurable pilot. For the one-role early-access offer, classify buyer readiness using three operational signals:

1. an active requisition;
2. a named pilot / decision owner;
3. a decision window for whether the workflow earns role #2.

Use these as implementation-readiness signals only:
- **0 / 3:** general staffing interest;
- **1–2 / 3:** partial readiness;
- **3 / 3:** design-partner-ready for a measurable one-role review.

This classification does not score a candidate, determine hiring suitability, validate a selection model, or guarantee pilot success. Consequential hiring judgment remains human-owned.

## Conversion implication

Do not label every staffing signup a design partner. Preserve low-friction early access for general interest, but reserve the explicit design-partner review CTA for buyers who provide all three readiness signals.

## Measurement implication

For attributable recruiter submissions, record `readiness_count` and `readiness_class` alongside the existing acquisition fields. Historical events are not rewritten.
