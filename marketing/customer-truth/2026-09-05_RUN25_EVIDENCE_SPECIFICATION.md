# ProofBerry Customer Truth — Run 25

## Thesis

**Skills-first hiring still needs an evidence specification.**

The market is moving toward skills-based hiring, while staffing platforms are automating more sourcing, screening, and client-submission work. That makes the next buyer question narrower and more operational: before a candidate is scored or summarized, what evidence should the client actually be able to inspect for this role?

A useful one-role pilot should define, before evaluation:

1. the role criterion;
2. acceptable evidence for that criterion;
3. the follow-up question when evidence is incomplete;
4. the recruiter reviewer note and uncertainty;
5. the human decision owner.

This is stronger than a generic "skills-first" claim because it converts skills-first hiring into a reviewable operating artifact.

## Current market evidence

- NACE Job Outlook 2026 reporting says use of skills-based hiring practices increased to nearly 70%, most often during interviewing and screening.
- SHRM's 2026 coverage of skills-based hiring emphasizes moving from credentials to competency and treating degrees/resumes as incomplete signals of readiness.
- Bullhorn's May 28, 2026 Amplify Digital Workers launch says staffing software can now automate screening and even build/send client-ready candidate submission packets.

## Product-truth boundary

The connected ProofBerry Base44 app currently has **0 RubricCriterion records**. Therefore Run 25 does not market a finalized live rubric or validated scoring system. The evidence-specification card is an illustrative design-partner worksheet for defining a one-role pilot before criteria are configured.

Do not claim validated predictive accuracy, compliance, bias elimination, guaranteed placement outcomes, automated hiring, or live ATS integration unless independently verified.
