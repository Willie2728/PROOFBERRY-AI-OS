## Customer Truth

**An identity check is not a capability check.**

Identity and fraud controls can help answer who entered the process and whether the presented person or records are authentic. A client still needs a different hiring question answered: what role-relevant evidence can I inspect, what remains uncertain, and what should I ask next?

ProofBerry should not position itself as an identity-verification product unless that capability is independently implemented and verified. The design-partner wedge is the capability-evidence layer beside the existing staffing workflow:

`role criterion → work artifact/demonstration → follow-up reasoning → recruiter reviewer note → open question → human decision owner`

## Buyer implication

The strongest near-term staffing CTA is not "replace your stack" or "trust our score." It is:

**Bring one live role where résumé polish is not enough. Compare the normal client handoff with an evidence-enhanced one.**

## Claims boundary

Do not claim that ProofBerry verifies identity, eliminates fraud or bias, guarantees compliance, predicts job performance, improves placements, or makes hiring decisions. Human reviewers retain consequential judgment.
