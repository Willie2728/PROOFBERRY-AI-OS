# ProofBerry AI — Customer Truth Run 19

## Thesis

**From candidate packet to evidence packet.**

Staffing technology is moving deeper into repetitive recruiting workflow. The differentiation opportunity for ProofBerry is not to claim it can make another prettier submittal. It is to help the recruiter hand a client more inspectable, role-relevant evidence inside the handoff while the consequential decision remains human-owned.

## Fresh market signals — verified September 5, 2026

- American Staffing Association, June 4, 2026: recruiter call time reached 286 minutes per week in Q1 2026, the highest level reported, while recruiters were also using more AI tools. This supports a relationship/judgment wedge rather than a spam-volume wedge.
- Bullhorn, May 28, 2026: Amplify Digital Workers included Enrich, Match, Screen, Outreach, and Present; Present was described as able to prepare/send client-ready candidate submissions. That means submittal preparation itself is increasingly automatable.
- Robert Half, March 10, 2026: 65% of surveyed hiring managers said AI-enhanced applications made skills harder to verify; 67% of surveyed HR leaders used staffing firms, and 89% of those staffing-firm users said those partners were effective at addressing AI-related hiring challenges.

## Customer problem

A client-ready submission can still leave the hiring client asking:

- Which role criterion does this evidence support?
- What did the candidate actually produce or explain?
- What uncertainty remains?
- What follow-up question should a human ask?
- Who owns the final decision?

## ProofBerry opportunity

Complement the existing staffing handoff with an **illustrative evidence packet**:

`role criterion → candidate artifact/evidence → follow-up reasoning → recruiter reviewer note → open question/uncertainty → human decision owner`

## Buyer wedge

Do not ask the staffing firm to replace its recruiting stack. Start with **one live role**, agree the evidence questions, review a small candidate cohort, and decide whether the client handoff became more inspectable.

## Claims boundary

Do not claim automatic hiring, placement-speed improvements, bias elimination, legal compliance, predictive validity, verified integrations, or customer outcomes without evidence.
