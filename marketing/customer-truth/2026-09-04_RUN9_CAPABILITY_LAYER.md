# ProofBerry Customer Truth — Run 9: Capability Layer

## Signal
Hiring trust is becoming a multi-layer problem: identity/fraud controls, AI-assisted matching, and role-relevant capability evidence are related but distinct.

## Current evidence
- Robert Half (March 10, 2026) reported 67% of surveyed U.S. HR leaders said AI-generated applications were slowing hiring, and 65% of hiring managers said AI-enhanced resumes made candidate skills harder to verify.
  Source: https://press.roberthalf.com/2026-03-10-Robert-Half-survey-67-of-HR-leaders-report-AI-generated-applications-are-slowing-hiring
- Greenhouse Real Talent describes three trust layers in its current product: fraud/spam detection, AI-assisted talent matching, and identity verification, while explicitly keeping hiring decisions with recruiters.
  Source: https://www.greenhouse.com/product-features/greenhouse-real-talent
- SHRM Labs' 2026 assessment-design coverage highlights the growing importance of scenario-based work and follow-up explanation when polished or AI-assisted answers are easy to generate.
  Source: https://www.shrm.org/labs/resources/when-candidates-can-fake-skills-real-time-assessment-design

## ProofBerry opportunity
Do not market ProofBerry as an identity-verification or fraud-detection replacement. Position it as the complementary capability-evidence layer: structured explanations, demonstrations, role-relevant work, and inspectable evidence that support human hiring decisions.

## Message to test
**Identity helps establish who. Matching helps organize where. Capability evidence helps reveal what someone can do.**

## Guardrails
- Never imply identity verification is unnecessary.
- Never claim ProofBerry detects fraud unless a verified product feature exists.
- Never imply automatic hiring or rejection.
- Keep decision authority human-owned.
- Treat competitor features as market evidence, not as claims of inferiority.
