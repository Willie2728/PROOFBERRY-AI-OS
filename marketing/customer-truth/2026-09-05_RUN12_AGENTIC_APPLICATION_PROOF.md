# ProofBerry Customer Truth — Run 12

## Signal

The job-search workflow itself is becoming more agentic.

Greenhouse reported on May 7, 2026 that, in a survey of more than 2,950 active job seekers, **30% said they were already using AI agents to search job openings, submit applications, or schedule interviews**.

Source: https://www.greenhouse.com/newsroom/greenhouse-launches-mcp-server

This compounds an already-documented verification problem. Robert Half reported on March 10, 2026 that **65% of surveyed U.S. hiring managers said AI-enhanced applications made candidate skills harder to verify**, while 67% of surveyed HR leaders said reviewing AI-generated applications was slowing hiring.

Source: https://press.roberthalf.com/2026-03-10-Robert-Half-survey-67-of-HR-leaders-report-AI-generated-applications-are-slowing-hiring

Greenhouse's current hiring-product direction also separates automation from consequential judgment: matching, identity/fraud signals and structured interviews can support the process while hiring decisions remain human-owned.

## Run 12 thesis

**Agentic application makes application administration more abundant. Candidate-owned, role-relevant evidence becomes more valuable.**

ProofBerry should not market against legitimate AI assistance. The useful wedge is the gap that remains after search/application logistics become easier to automate:

1. What does this role actually require?
2. What evidence can the candidate explain, demonstrate and stand behind?
3. Can a human reviewer inspect that evidence without treating every applicant as a fraud case?

## Claims boundary

Do not claim that ProofBerry eliminates fraud, verifies identity, guarantees better hires, guarantees candidate visibility, or makes autonomous hiring decisions. Do not imply that using an AI job-search agent is inherently dishonest.

## Conversion implication

Run 12 creative should sell **candidate-owned proof**, not more application automation. Distribution should remain attributable to qualified waitlist actions rather than vanity reach.
