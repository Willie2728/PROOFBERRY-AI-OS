# ProofBerry Customer Truth — Run 23: One Role, One Owner

Date: 2026-09-05

## Market evidence

Bullhorn's 2026 recruitment-industry research says only a minority of firms have AI embedded throughout the workflow; data quality and security remain major adoption barriers, and 20% of staffing leaders say they lack a clear implementation plan. Bullhorn also describes firms using internal AI ambassadors to make early wins relatable across the organization.

Source: https://www.bullhorn.com/grid/2026-industry-trends/report/

American Staffing Association's July 27, 2026 AI Acceleration guidance emphasizes clean data, training people to use AI outputs intelligently, and instrumenting results so teams can course-correct.

Source: https://americanstaffing.net/posts/2026/07/27/ai-acceleration/

## Customer Truth

A low-risk pilot still fails if nobody owns it.

ProofBerry's staffing design-partner motion should make implementation ownership explicit before the first candidate:

**one live role → one named pilot owner → one client-side reviewer → one scorecard → one decision date**

This extends the Run 22 parallel-run proposition without requiring ATS replacement or a broad transformation commitment.

## Claim boundary

Do not claim ProofBerry guarantees integrations, ROI, placement speed, compliance, bias elimination, predictive validity, or successful hiring outcomes. Consequential hiring decisions remain human-owned.
