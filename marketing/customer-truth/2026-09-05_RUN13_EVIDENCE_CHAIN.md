# ProofBerry Growth Run 13 — Customer Truth: Evidence Chain

## Market signal

Hiring-assessment vendors are moving beyond generic résumé screening toward role-specific evidence, traceable scoring and human review.

- Workera announced proctored hiring assessments on July 28, 2026 that use tasks intended to mirror the job and produce evidence for high-stakes hiring review: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/workera-introduces-proctored-assessments-to-power-high-stakes-hiring-decisions-302836173.html
- FairShot currently positions adaptive interviews around role cases, competencies and criteria, with transcript-linked evidence and recruiters retaining the decision: https://fairshot.tech/
- The Cigna Group's March 2026 AI-use notice says AI-enabled tools may assist hiring while recruitment remains human-led and people make progression decisions: https://jobs.thecignagroup.com/ai-use

## Customer truth

The market is not only asking, “Can AI score candidates?” The stronger enterprise question is becoming:

**What evidence produced the score, how does it relate to the role, and can a human inspect it?**

That creates a useful ProofBerry wedge. ProofBerry should not compete on generic AI scoring alone. Its defensible message is an inspectable evidence chain:

**role criterion → observed candidate evidence → structured score/context → human review.**

## Audience pain

Recruiter / workforce buyer:
- Too many tools compress nuanced candidate information into a number.
- A number is difficult to defend internally if the reviewer cannot see what produced it.
- Hiring managers need concise evidence, not another opaque ranking.

Candidate:
- A score without inspectable context can feel final and arbitrary.
- Candidates benefit when capability is represented through role-relevant work, explanations and artifacts rather than generic polish alone.

## Run 13 message

**A hiring score without evidence is just another number.**

Supporting line:

**Role criteria. Candidate evidence. Human review.**

## Claims boundary

Do not claim ProofBerry:
- eliminates bias;
- guarantees fair hiring;
- is legally compliant in every jurisdiction;
- predicts job performance with a validated accuracy percentage;
- automatically makes hiring decisions;
- prevents fraud or cheating.

Use “built to,” “designed to,” and “helps make evidence easier to inspect” unless a product behavior is directly verified.
