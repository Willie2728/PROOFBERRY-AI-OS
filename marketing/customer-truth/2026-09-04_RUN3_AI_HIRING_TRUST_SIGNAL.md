# ProofBerry Growth Run 3 — Customer Truth: AI-Assisted Hiring + Trust

Date: 2026-09-04
Primary objective: qualified ProofBerry early-access waitlist signups.

## Strongest current signal
LinkedIn's January 7, 2026 talent research reports a two-sided market problem: US applicants per open role have doubled since spring 2022, 66% of recruiters say finding qualified talent has become harder, 93% plan to increase AI use in 2026, 59% say AI is already helping them uncover candidates with skills they otherwise may not have found, and 66% plan to increase AI use for pre-screening interviews.

Source: https://news.linkedin.com/2026/LinkedIn-Research-Talent-2026

Greenhouse's 2026 AI-in-hiring research adds a trust signal: 46% of job seekers said trust in hiring had declined, 42% pointed to automation as the reason, and 87% wanted disclosure when automation is used.

Source surfaced through Greenhouse: https://www.linkedin.com/posts/greenhouse-inc-_the-2026-ai-in-hiring-report-activity-7432105515587084288-eTp3

## Message implications
1. Candidate pain is not merely "find more jobs." It is "stand out and be understood in a process that is increasingly AI-assisted."
2. Recruiter pain is not merely "get more applicants." It is "find clearer qualified signal inside high application volume."
3. Trust matters. ProofBerry marketing should explicitly preserve human decision authority and avoid positioning AI as an opaque autonomous hiring judge.
4. ProofBerry's strongest category language remains: capability evidence / skills-verification layer for the agentic hiring era.

## New creative hypothesis
**Transparency + capability evidence will outperform fear-based ATS messaging.**

Instead of saying "AI is rejecting you," show the reality more accurately: automated systems may organize, summarize, match, or pre-screen candidates before a recruiter reviews them. ProofBerry helps candidates structure evidence so their capability can be understood by people and AI-assisted talent systems while the hiring decision remains human.

## Claims guardrail
Do not claim that every employer uses AI screening, that ProofBerry guarantees passage through an ATS, or that ProofBerry guarantees interviews/jobs. Use "may," "increasingly," "designed to," and evidence-backed market statistics with source/date when cited.
