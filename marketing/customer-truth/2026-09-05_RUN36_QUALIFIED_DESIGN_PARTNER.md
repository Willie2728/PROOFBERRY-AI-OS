# ProofBerry AI — Run 36 Customer Truth

Date: 2026-09-05

## Customer Truth

**A waitlist lead is not the same thing as a design partner.**

ProofBerry has enough unmeasured awareness inventory. The next useful staffing signal is whether an interested buyer has the minimum operating conditions for a measurable one-role comparison:

1. one active requisition;
2. one named staffing-side pilot owner;
3. one decision window for whether the workflow earns a second role.

These are qualification signals, not guarantees of pilot success or hiring outcomes.

## Market context

SHRM's 2026 State of AI in HR research reports that many HR functions still do not formally measure the success of AI investments. That supports defining an evaluation contract before expansion, not assuming that AI adoption itself is success.

Use this only as category evidence. Do not convert industry survey findings into a ProofBerry result.

## Growth implication

Optimize the recruiter path for **qualified design-partner intent**, not raw email volume. Preserve the current recruiting workflow for comparison; measure reviewer usefulness, recruiter effort, candidate friction, unresolved questions, and whether the buyer elects to test a second role. The consequential hiring decision remains human-owned.
