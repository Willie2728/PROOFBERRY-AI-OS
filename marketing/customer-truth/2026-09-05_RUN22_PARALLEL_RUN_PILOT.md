# ProofBerry Customer Truth — Run 22: Parallel-Run Pilot

Date: 2026-09-05
Audience: staffing firms / recruiting teams

## Market evidence

Bullhorn's 2026 GRID Industry Trends Report says only 10% of staffing firms report AI embedded throughout their workflow, while fewer than half use AI for any individual recruitment function. That means most buyers are still in partial adoption rather than full-stack replacement mode.

ManpowerGroup's March 3, 2026 Hubert partnership also reinforces a human-first pattern: AI can handle early information collection while recruiters remain at the center of hiring decisions.

Sources:
- https://www.bullhorn.com/grid/2026-industry-trends/report/
- https://www.manpowergroup.com/news-releases/news/manpowergroup-scales-human-first-ai-interviewing-to-address-global-talent-shortage

## Customer Truth synthesis

The next ProofBerry staffing pitch should not ask a firm to replace its ATS, submission workflow, or recruiter judgment before value is demonstrated.

**Run one live role in parallel with the workflow the firm already uses.**

Keep the normal candidate submission. Add a ProofBerry evidence packet beside it. Compare whether the client review becomes clearer without creating unreasonable recruiter effort or candidate friction. Expand only if the workflow earns a second role.

This is an inference from the adoption evidence, not a claim that all staffing firms prefer parallel pilots.

## Conversion implication

Lead with lower adoption risk:

`Keep your stack. Run one role in parallel.`

The buyer is being asked to test one observable workflow outcome, not commit to a platform migration.

## Claim boundaries

Do not promise ATS integration, placement speed, hiring accuracy, bias elimination, ROI, or automatic hiring decisions. Describe PB-DOC-009 as an illustrative early-access design-partner concept until the exact pilot workflow is agreed with a real buyer.
