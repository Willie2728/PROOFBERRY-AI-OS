# Growth Run 11 — Customer Truth: Proof Without Suspicion

## Market signal

ProofBerry should not position stronger hiring signal as a license for suspicion-first hiring. The same AI adoption that increases recruiter workload is also creating candidate-trust problems when evaluation is opaque.

Fresh evidence used this run:

- Greenhouse Candidate AI Interview Report, published May 1, 2026: among 2,950 active job seekers surveyed, 63% had experienced an AI interview; 70% said they were not clearly told upfront that AI would evaluate them; 38% said they had walked away from a hiring process because it included an AI interview. Source: https://www.greenhouse.com/newsroom/63-of-job-seekers-have-faced-an-ai-interview-most-havent-had-a-good-one-yet
- Robert Half, published March 10, 2026: 67% of surveyed U.S. HR leaders said AI-generated applications were slowing hiring; 65% of hiring managers said AI-enhanced resumes made skills harder to verify. Source: https://press.roberthalf.com/2026-03-10-Robert-Half-survey-67-of-HR-leaders-report-AI-generated-applications-are-slowing-hiring
- SHRM 2026 Talent Trends, published April 27, 2026: nearly 70% of HR professionals reported challenges recruiting for full-time positions, with SHRM advocating skills-based talent strategies. Source: https://www.shrm.org/about/press-room/shrm-unveils-2026-talent-trends-report--data-driven-insights-for

## Customer truth

Recruiters need better signal. Candidates also need to know what is being evaluated and why. A product that improves evidence quality while preserving transparency can occupy a more defensible position than either resume-only screening or fraud-first surveillance.

## Run 11 thesis

**Better hiring signal should not require treating every applicant like a fraud case.**

ProofBerry should frame its role as:

1. define the work that matters for the role;
2. tell candidates what evidence is expected;
3. let candidates explain, do, and show relevant work;
4. give the hiring team inspectable evidence;
5. keep consequential hiring decisions human-owned.

## Claims guardrail

Do not claim ProofBerry eliminates fraud, guarantees better hires, proves identity, or makes automated hiring decisions. Do not repeat survey statistics without source attribution. Do not imply that use of AI by a candidate is itself dishonest.
