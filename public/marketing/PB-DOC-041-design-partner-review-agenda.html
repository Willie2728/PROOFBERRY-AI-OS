Know what the first review conversation is for before you book it.

Use this agenda after you have a live role, an accountable human owner, a success rule and a proposed evidence standard. It is designed to keep a design-partner conversation focused on one bounded review—not on a generic AI demo.

One live roleOne accountable ownerOne evidence ruleOne next-step decision

1Confirm the role and decision

Name the live role, who owns the consequential hiring decision, and what decision the review is meant to inform. Do not let the software become the decision owner.

2Inspect the proposed evidence packet

Review what would count as candidate evidence: a work artifact, the candidate's actual contribution, material AI/tool assistance, corroboration or an external outcome receipt when legitimately available, plus unresolved questions.

3Name the comparison baseline

Decide what the one-role review would be compared against—your current staffing process, current tool/workflow, manual review, or a baseline still to be defined.

4Set the handoff and retention boundaries

State where the evidence must land to be useful and how long it should remain available. These are buyer requirements to validate, not claims that an integration, deletion schedule or compliance control already exists.

5Predeclare the stop condition

Identify what would make the review stop or return to a human—for example missing consent, unresolved provenance, unavailable evidence, policy conflict or an out-of-scope request.

6Decide what earns role #2

Leave the conversation with a buyer-defined rule for continuing, pausing or rejecting the next test. A completed form, document open or successful build does not count as proof of product performance.

Bring these six items

  • One live role or requisition.
  • The accountable human decision owner.
  • The minimum evidence packet you would want to inspect.
  • The current comparison baseline.
  • The destination and retention expectation for evidence.
  • The buyer-defined rule that would make a second-role test worthwhile.
Decision rule: the first conversation is successful when the buyer and ProofBerry can state what will be reviewed, who remains accountable, what evidence is required, what boundary must be validated, and what observation would justify a next step. It does not mean a pilot has been accepted, scheduled, deployed or proven effective.

This is a planning artifact, not a hiring recommendation, legal opinion, compliance certification, candidate score, pilot acceptance, product-performance result or service-level commitment. Do not include passwords, API keys, protected health information, government identifiers or unnecessary candidate-sensitive data in planning notes.
