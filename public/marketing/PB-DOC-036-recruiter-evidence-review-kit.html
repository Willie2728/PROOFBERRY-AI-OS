Decision rule

Book the conversation only when the buyer can name the role, the evidence rule, the human owner, and the unresolved risks.

ProofBerry is being built to make role-relevant evidence easier to inspect and to preserve accountable human ownership of consequential hiring decisions. These artifacts are buyer-scoping aids. They are not candidate verification, legal advice, a bias audit, a compliance certification, a validated selection procedure, pilot acceptance, or evidence of a hiring outcome.

Scope one live role review →

Why this format: B2B buyers increasingly research independently, while still turning to sellers when they need validation and decision confidence. ProofBerry’s buyer path should therefore provide substantive self-service evidence before asking for contact information or a sales conversation.
